# annot-092 review findings

## pass-4-q4

- *les parens des meurtris, offenſez, reçoyuent quelque allegement* is rendered "the kinsfolk of the murdered and the injured may receive". Callistratus (D. 48.19.28.15: *solacio sit cognatis et adfinibus interemptorum*) suggests *offenſez* qualifies the kinsfolk ("the kinsfolk of the murdered, [thus] wronged") rather than adding a class; the French comma allows either. Left for the reviewer.
