# texte-44 review findings

## pass-2-q2

- R&L put the oath-offer in Bertrande's mouth: '(telling whoever was willing to believe her word that she would undergo a thousand cruel deaths rather than swear …)'. The French *qui l'en voulut croire à ſon ſerment, ſe ſouſmettãt à mille morts cruelles, ſi elle iuroit* has du Tilh offering to abide by her oath and staking his life on it; she then refuses to swear 'nor believe him upon it either' (*ni l'en croire auſsi*). Ours is right.
