# texte-40 review findings

## pass-2-q2

- Consistency: *ſoubredens* → supernumerary teeth, as *ſoubre-dents* at texte-61 (glossary decision pass-2-q2).: two extra teeth in the upper jaw → two supernumerary teeth in the upper jaw
- R&L: 'two broken teeth in his lower jaw'. French *deux ſoubredens à la machoire de deſſus* = two supernumerary teeth in the upper jaw; R&L are wrong on both the kind of tooth and the jaw. R&L 'an ingrown nail on a forefinger' for *vne ongle du premier doigt enfoncée* is interpretive; ours ('sunken') is literal.
