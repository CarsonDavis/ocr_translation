# texte-67 review findings

## pass-3-q3

- *luy eſtant en preuention de meſme faict deuant le Seneſchal* — both we ("for the same deed") and R&L ("for the same reason") read it literally, but texte-47 says the Seneschal's prosecution was on other charges (*d'autres cas*). Coras's own inconsistency; left as printed, worth a note in the apparatus.
