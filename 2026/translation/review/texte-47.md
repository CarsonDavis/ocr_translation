# texte-47 review findings

## pass-2-q2

- R&L: 'released … by virtue of the reversed judgment of the Seneschal' for *l'apointement de contraires* (an interlocutory order, not a reversal) — already noted in §2/§5; ours is right. R&L also reverse the direction of *ſouſminiſtrant touſiours par deſſouz main toute faueur & aide ledit Pierre Guerre* ('the Cadet … acting always under the authority and protection and with the help of the said Pierre Guerre'): it is Pierre Guerre who underhandedly supplies favour and aid. R&L omit *on luy auança ce fait auſsi* (this charge too was advanced against him).
