# texte-53 review findings

## pass-2-q2

- R&L: 'they were promptly and validly excused, and the reasons found good and well-proved' — *reprochez* is 'objected to' (§5), and *les obiects* are the grounds of objection, not 'reasons'; the same error recurs in their texte-54 ('the witnesses have been validly excused'). Ours is right in both.
