# texte-80 review findings

## pass-3-q3

- Fidelity: *de tous vniquement receu, & recognu pour Martin Guerre* — *vniquement* qualifies both verbs (received and recognised by all as the one and only Martin Guerre); "received by all as the only one" splits the phrase and leaves "the only one" dangling.: and the newcomer received by all as the only one, and recognised as Martin Guerre → and the newcomer received and recognised by all as the sole Martin Guerre
- R&L divergences in the decision, all theirs: "the appeal … has been granted, performed and nullified" garbles the formula *a mis, & met l'appellation … & ce dont a eſté appelé, au neant* (set aside as null, §5); "the Court" for *à Iuſtice* (ours: "of Justice"); "executioner of the High Court" for *executeur de la haute Iuſtice* (high justice, not the court); "Legally pronounced" for *Prononce iudicialement*. R&L do not translate the display head EXPOSITION DES Paroles de l'arreſt or the one-word TEXTE heads (texte-81–84).
