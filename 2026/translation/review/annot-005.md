# Findings: annot-005 (pass-0-glossary)

## Changed

- French: *d'impoſer à tous les parens*
  - was: to impose upon all the relatives
  - now: to impose upon all the kinsfolk
  - why: *parens* → kinsfolk
- French: *A M. Antoine, en ſon triumuirat*
  - was: To M. Antony, in his triumvirate
  - now: To Mark Antony, in his triumvirate
  - why: Roman praenomina expanded (decision log); the section already had 'Mark Antony' a few lines on
- French: *Quoy entendu par M. Antoine*
  - was: Which being discovered by M. Antony
  - now: Which being discovered by Mark Antony
  - why: as above
- French: *la femme de Q. Sertorius*
  - was: the wife of Q. Sertorius
  - now: the wife of Quintus Sertorius
  - why: praenomina expanded
- French: *Loys viues homme bien lettré*
  - was: Louis Vives, a man well lettered
  - now: Juan Luis Vives, a man well lettered
  - why: standard English form per §7; the Notes already identify him so
- French: *trois mille ſept cens cinquante eſcus / vintcinq eſcuz / 3750. eſcus*
  - was: … écus (roman, three times)
  - now: … *écus* (italic)
  - why: money words italicised, §8; annot-053 and annot-109 already italicise *écus*
- French: *vne effigie de ſainct Iean*
  - was: an image of St John
  - now: an image of Saint John
  - why: the print's *ſainct* spelled out → Saint, its *S.* → St (decision log)

## Flagged, not changed

- *Bruxelles, ville du dioceſe de Spire* is rendered 'Bruxelles' — Münster's town in the diocese of Speyer is Bruchsal (Latin *Bruxella*), not Brussels; a fidelity point for pass 1, not changed here.
- *nul ne ſçait (comme ie croy) l'hiſtoire de Iacob*: the translator's [unclear] stands; recorded in the misreadings list together with the parallel at annot-104.
