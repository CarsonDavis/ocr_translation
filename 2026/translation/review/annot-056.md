# annot-056 review findings

## pass-3-q3

- Coras writes *le priſonnier* for the man brought from the Basque country at two, i.e. Martin Guerre, not du Tilh; translated as printed. *diſertement* is "distinctly" here (an infant's articulation) against §9's "eloquently" at annot-108; the contexts differ and both stand.
