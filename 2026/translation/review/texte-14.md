# Findings: texte-14 (pass-0-glossary)

## Changed

- French: *pourſuyui de ſes propres parens*
  - was: prosecuted by his own relatives
  - now: prosecuted by his own kinsfolk
  - why: *parens* → kinsfolk

## Flagged, not changed

- None.

## Ringold–Lewis

- Ringold and Lewis: 'had once already been brought to court … and the account cleared and the remaining sum paid before the said judge of Rieux' — the French (*mis en inſtance, pour raiſon d'iceluy & rendu compte, & preſtation de reliqua*) says the suit was *for* the account and the balance, not that they had been paid; ours is right.
