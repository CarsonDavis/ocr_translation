## pass-4-q4

- Fidelity: *voire que ne l'ayent voulu punir d'vn ſimple banniſſement* intensifies "not capital" — the ordinary Roman penalty was relegation, capital only where incest was joined (note d); the English inverted it so that incest earned mere banishment.: nay, that they did not wish to punish it with a simple banishment, unless when the adultery was joined with incest → nay, that they wished to punish it only with a simple banishment, unless when the adultery was joined with incest
- [unclear] left on *ſemblẽt peu, ou point meriter quelque excuſe*: the opinion Coras reports (and then rejects as impious) needs "seem to deserve some excuse"; the whole book offers no parallel to fix the dropped word. Possibly *peine* fell out before *quelque excuſe* ("little or no penalty, [but] some excuse").
