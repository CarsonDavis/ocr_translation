# annot-036 review findings

## pass-2-q2

- *Par ces lettres, & caracteres faut entendre que Blaſpheme, eſt vn nom tiré du Grec*: the capital P may open a new sentence ('By these letters and characters [of the Greek] it must be understood …') rather than close the Tetragrammaton clause as the English takes it. Either construal is defensible; left.
