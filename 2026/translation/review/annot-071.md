## sweep-opus

- [check 5] Negation reversed without a note. French: 'quelques vns en mettent vn autre, qui s'appelle Pharmacie {e}, mal toutesfois à mon aduis, attendu qu'elle ne consiste point seulement en drogues, bruuages, & empoisonnemens' (= 'does not consist only in drugs'). English: 'wrongly, however, in my opinion, seeing that it consists in nothing but drugs, potions and poisonings'. The English sense ('only drugs, so not magic') fits the concession that follows ('Ie ne veux pourtant nier que ... Pharmacie ne peust estre colloquee parmy les especes de Magie'), so it may be a deliberate correction of the print, but the section Notes do not record it. Either add a note/[bracket] saying the print reads 'ne ... point seulement', or render literally.
