# annot-020 review findings

## pass-1-q1

- Consistency: *femme de bien, & honneſte* as at texte-20/texte-39 (§9 *homme de bien* → man of worth).: she ought to be committed to some good and honest woman until the end of the suit → she ought to be committed to some woman of worth and honest until the end of the suit

## sweep-opus

- Grammar slip introduced by review pass 1 (f7e42b3): replacing 'good and honest woman' with the glossary 'woman of worth' left 'honest' dangling with no noun ('committed to some woman of worth and honest until the end of the suit'). Punctuating it as an apposition, as the French does (*quelque femme de bien, & honneſte*), restores the grammar and keeps the glossary rendering.: some woman of worth and honest until → some woman of worth, and honest, until
