⟦p158⟧Making them also executors of his

testament.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p158, after Annotation CX. (the 109th by the true count); it carries no marginal citations. It completes the disposition of texte-107: the two guardians, Jean du Tilh and Dominique Rebendaire, are also named executors, which Annotation CXI (annot-110) expounds. The print sets the clause as two display lines, dividing *te-|ſtament* at the line end with a hyphen; the transcription keeps the two lines as two paragraphs, and so does the English, but the word is not divided ("of his / testament"). *Executeurs de ſon teſtament* → "executors of his testament" (*teſtament* → testament, as throughout annot-109).
