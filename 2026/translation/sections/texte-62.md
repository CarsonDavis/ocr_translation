⟦p082⟧And as to saying that by the inquiries it is reported that the rumour at the said place of Artigat is that Pierre Guerre and his sons-in-law were constraining the said de Rols to carry on the prosecution: it is answered that proof by rumour and common repute is not received, save in certain cases which could not be made to fit here.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p082; the page carries no marginal citations. Reflow gap read through: *rap porté* (*rapporté*). *Et de dire, que* → "And as to the argument that" (the record's formula for taking up a point made for the appellant; cf. *Et quant aux autres marques* at texte-58); *enqueſtes* → "inquiries" per the procedure glossary; *le bruit eſtre audit lieu d'Artigat* → "the rumour at the said place of Artigat is" (as at texte-39, which records the same depositions); *gendres* → "sons-in-law"; *contraignoyent … faire la pourſuite* → "were constraining … to carry on the prosecution"; *la preuue par bruit & fame* → "proof by rumour and common repute" per the glossary; *n'eſt pas receuë* → "is not received"; *qui ne ſe pourroient accommoder ici* → "which could not be made to fit here". The cases excepted are those listed in Annotation LXI.
