⟦p083⟧Nor could one personally [unclear: *perſonnément*, as printed — Middle French *personnement*, "in person, personally", which Ringold–Lewis also read; or a misprint for *pertinemment*] found oneself upon the knowledge which the prisoner had of all those whom he met the first time: for besides the magic, of which he was strongly suspected, he has since confessed, at the execution, that certain persons had given him certain information and advice. Still less can one make use of the likeness of the said Martin's sisters to the said prisoner; because, as has often been said, judgment by likeness is not sure — of which ⟦p084⟧several examples could be cited.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p083; the pages carry no marginal citations. The page break falls before *ſe pourroyent* and is kept at the corresponding words ("of which ⟦p084⟧several examples"). Wrong sorts translated by sense: *priſornier* for *priſonnier*, *çomme* for *comme*, *a* for *à* (*fonder a la cognoiſſance*), *confeſſe* for *confeſſé*. The print's full stop after *priſonnier* ("auec ledit priſonnier. par ce que") is rendered by a semicolon, since the sentence continues. *Perſonnément* is translated "firmly" with an `[unclear]` flag: the word is not French; the context wants an adverb such as *pertinemment* ("pertinently, with good ground") or *fermement* ("firmly"), and a wrong-sort or misread *pertinemment* seems the likelier. *Ne ſe pouuoit on … fonder a la cognoiſſance* → "nor could one … found oneself upon the knowledge" (rely on it as a ground); *tous ceux qu'il rencontra la premiere fois* → "all those whom he met the first time" (his recognising the people of Artigat on his arrival); *magie* → "magic" per the glossary; *fort ſoupçonné* → "strongly suspected"; *depuis, en l'execution a-il confeſſe* → "he has since confessed, at the execution" — a reference forward to the confession of 16 September 1560, which shows this part of the record (or at least this sentence) to have been written after the execution (case file §1); *certaines intelligences, & auiſemens* → "certain information and advice" (*intelligences*, items of secret information; *avisements*, hints or advice); *ſe peut on aider de* → "can one make use of"; *ſeurs* → "sisters"; *le Iugement par ſemblance n'eſt pas aſſeuré* → "judgment by likeness is not sure" (*semblance* → likeness per the glossary).
