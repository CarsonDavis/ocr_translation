⟦p040⟧The matter having been set down in law upon the manner of proceeding, there follows an Order of confrontations against the said du Tilh; and nevertheless, that the said de Rols shall present herself in person to be heard, and confronted if need be; and that certain witnesses comprised and named in the hearing of the said du Tilh, calling himself Martin Guerre, and others who shall be delivered by declaration, shall be heard upon certain facts resulting from the case. The said de Rols, being heard, answers likewise, and agrees in everything with the answers of the said du Tilh; save that she adds that, a little after being married to Martin Guerre, they remained eight or nine years bound and bewitched [*liez & maleficiez*: under the spell of impotence, the *nouement d'aiguillette*], without being able to cohabit carnally together; wherefore her nearest kinsfolk counselled her to ask for a separation of marriage, to which however she would never listen.
