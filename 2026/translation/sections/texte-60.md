⟦p079⟧And as to the marks and scars imprinted on the eyes, forehead, hands and nails of the said du Tilh, prisoner, and formerly ⟦p080⟧recognised on the body of Martin Guerre: it will be answered that a part of these signs, such as the warts on the hands, the drops of blood in the eye, and the sinking of the nail, are each proved by only one witness, and thus they are singular witnesses, who make no proof even though they were a thousand, each deposing to his own fact.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p079, after Annotation LIX. **The section as stitched contains two things**: the record's paragraph on the marks and scars (p079–p080), and the whole of **Annotation LX** (p080–p081), whose heading the print sets as *ANNNT. LX.* — a wrong sort for *ANNOTAT. LX.* — which the stitcher did not recognise as a heading, so that no `annot-060` was cut for it and the section ids after it are shifted by one (the section `annot-060` in `text/sections.json` is headed *ANNOTAT. LXI.* in the print). The heading is translated on its own line as ANNOTATION LX, the French paragraphing kept. The page breaks fall between *iadis* and *recogneuës* ("formerly ⟦p080⟧recognised") and between *ceux* and *d'vne cité* ("all those ⟦p081⟧of a city"). The record's paragraph carries no marginal citation; the annotation's marker alphabet restarts at *a* and runs a–ſ (no *j*): the body carries a, b, c, d, e, f, h, i, k, l, m, n, o, p, q, r, ſ, and the margin carries notes under the same keys and one more, *g*, which has no marker in the body (the transcription marks it an orphan). By its content (Butrigarius on the Code's law against the Arians) note *g* belongs to the sentence on proof of heresy by two singular witnesses, between {f} and {h}; it is listed below as an orphan at its evident place. The `⟨alt⟩` choices in the body: *emprainctes⟨alt:empraintes⟩* is taken as either, both readings being *empreintes* ("imprinted"); *meſchancetè⟨alt:meſchanceté⟩* is taken as either, both being *meschanceté*, the accent alone differing; *e.⟨alt:e-⟩ſtre* is taken as B, *e-ſtre*, the word *eſtre* broken at the line end with a hyphen, a point there being no sense; *prudence,⟨alt:prudence;⟩* is taken as A, the comma, the participle *traitez & diffiniz* continuing the clause. In the margin: note *e*, *c.⟨alt:[?].⟩ ij.* is taken as A, *c.* (*capitulum*, "the second chapter *Qualiter*"), and *Balde⟨alt:[?]alde⟩* as A, *Balde* (Baldus); note *f*, *en⟨alt:e[?]⟩ ſes deſi-⟨alt:desi-⟩ſions* is taken as A for both markers (one line, one choice), reading A being complete and legible and both giving *en ſes deciſions*; note *i*, *cõſil⟨alt:cõſeil⟩* is taken as either, both being *conseil* (*consilium*), though note *o* spells it *cõſeil*; note *k*, *nihilomi⟨alt:nibilomi⟩nus* is taken as A (*Nihilominus*, the chapter's incipit, which note *ſ* repeats), *auſſi⟨alt:auſsi⟩* as either (one word), and *iij.⟨alt:ij.⟩ q. ix.* as A (*Causa* 3, *quaestio* 9 — *Causa* 2 of the Decretum has only eight *quaestiones*); note *n*, *c.⟨alt:e.⟩ vbi periculum* is taken as A, *c.* (*capitulum*); note *ſ*, *preallé⟨alt:preallé-⟩gué* is taken as either, both being *preallegué*. Reflow gaps read through: *execra ble*, *veu lent*, *grã de*; in the margin *te ſti.*, *li cet*, *preal legue*, *alle gué*, *nihilomi nus*, *Bella per.*, *commencé ment*. The sentence at {h} is defective in the print: "bien que l'vn d'eux depoſent d'vne eſpece d'hereſies, diſent ils, combien que ſoyent par diuers noms deſignées, ſont neantmoins entreliees" lacks the second limb (that the other witness deposes to another kind) and the connective before *hereſies*; it is translated literally, with "[and the other to another]" supplied in brackets and a colon marking the break; see the report. The verb elided after *peuuent ſuffiſamment* is supplied as "[prove]", and "[those]" supplies the noun after *comme de pluſieurs*. In the record's paragraph: *marques, & cicatrices* → "marks and scars" per the glossary; *emprainctes* → "imprinted"; *verrues* → "warts" per the glossary (texte-38, annot-040); *goutes de ſang à l'œil* → "drops of blood in the eye" (the mark at texte-38); *enfoncement de l'ongle* → "the sinking of the nail" (the nail grown into the finger, texte-38); *teſmoins ſinguliers* → "singular witnesses" per the glossary (annot-032), the *testes singulares* who each depose to a different fact; *depoſans chacun de ſon faict* → "each deposing to his own fact".
