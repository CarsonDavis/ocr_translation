⟦p128⟧Executor of high justice,

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p128; it is the words *Executeur de la haute iuſtice* from the *arrest* (texte-77), a clause left open by its comma, and carries no marginal citations. In texte-77 the phrase was rendered "the executioner", per the glossary (§5 and §9: *executeur de la haute justice* → executioner, "literally 'executor of high justice'"). Here the literal rendering is used instead, because the following annotation (annot-090) is an exposition of the words themselves — what *la haute justice* is, and why the hangmen are called its *executeurs* in France — and "Executioner" alone would leave that annotation without its subject. Flagged in the report for the reviewer to decide whether the two places should be brought into line.
