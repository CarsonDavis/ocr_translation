⟦p129⟧It was also fitting that this abominable and prodigious impostor, the infamous minister of the ruin of the house of the said Martin, should be executed before that house where he had committed, and so long continued, his treachery {a2}. Whereupon the words of Callistratus the Jurisconsult are worthy to be transcribed here. BRIGANDS and notorious thieves, says he, ought to be hanged in the place where they have practised their brigandage, so that the others, seeing them, may be turned away from like evil deeds, and the kinsfolk of the murdered and the injured may receive some relief and consolation, seeing justice justly rendered, and the penalty executed, in the place of the evil deed {b}.

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOT. XCII. on p129; the body carries two markers, {a2} and {b}, both on p129, served by the two keyed notes (the transcription keys the first *a2* because p129 already carries Annotation XCI's note *a*; the print has plain letters). Wrong sorts and slips translated by sense: *de'Calliſtrat* for *de Calliſtrat*; *Iuriſconſute* for *Iuriſconſulte*; *brigaudage* for *brigandage*; *ſemblable malefices* for *ſemblables malefices*. The print's full stop after *tranſcrites* and the capitals of *LES brigans* opening the quotation are kept. *Prodigieux impoſteur* → "prodigious impostor" per the glossary; *miniſtre infame de la ruine* → "the infamous minister of the ruin" (*ministre* in the sense of agent); *prodition* → "treachery" (Latin *proditio*; cf. *proditoirement* → treacherously); *Calliſtrat, Iuriſconſute* → "Callistratus the Jurisconsult" per the glossary (*Jurisconsultes*); *brigans, & fameux larrons* → "brigands and notorious thieves" (Callistratus's *famosos latrones*; *larron* → thief per the glossary); *pendus* → "hanged" (Callistratus's *furca figendos*, fastened to the fork-gibbet); *brigaudage* → "brigandage"; *malefices* → "evil deeds" per the glossary; *les parens des meurtris, offenſez* → "the kinsfolk of the murdered and the injured" (*cognatis et adfinibus interemptorum*; *parens* → kinsfolk per the glossary); *allegement & conſolation* → "relief and consolation" (*solacio*); *au lieu du malefice* → "in the place of the evil deed".

- {a2} (p129): **Digest 48.19.28 (*De poenis*, l. *Capitalium*, Callistratus), § *Famosos* (D. 48.19.28.15); and the Authentica *Qua in provincia* inserted in Code 3.15 (*Ubi de criminibus agi oportet*), drawn from Novel 69** — l. capitaliũ. P. famoſos. D. de pœn. Aut. qua in prouin cia. C. vbi de crim. ag. op. [The *Authentica*: in whatever province a man offends, there let him be punished — the rule that the penalty is executed where the crime was committed.]
- {b} (p129): **The same § *Famosos* (D. 48.19.28.15), cited above** — P. famoſos. allegué. [*Famosos latrones in his locis, ubi grassati sunt, furca figendos compluribus placuit, ut et conspectu deterreantur alii ab isdem facinoribus et solacio sit cognatis et adfinibus interemptorum eodem loco poena reddita, in quo latrones homicidia fecissent* — the passage Coras transcribes.]
