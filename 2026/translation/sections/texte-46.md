⟦p067⟧Complaining moreover to several that the said Pierre Guerre and his wife, mother of the said de Rols, wished to force and constrain her to accuse the said prisoner and to say that he was not her husband — even to threatening to turn her out of the house if she did not say it.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p067, after Annotation XLV; the section carries no marginal citations. The paragraph is a participial continuation of texte-43 (*Se plaignant en outre*, "Complaining moreover"), and is kept so. The print's *ladi.* is its abbreviation of *ladite*; reflow gap read through: *iuſ qu'à* (*iuſqu'à*). *Forcer & contraindre* → "force and constrain"; *ſa femme, mere de ladite de Rols* → "his wife, mother of the said de Rols" (Pierre Guerre had married Bertrande's widowed mother, per the case file §3); *la tirer hors de la maiſon* → "to turn her out of the house"; *ſi ne le diſoit* → "if she did not say it".
