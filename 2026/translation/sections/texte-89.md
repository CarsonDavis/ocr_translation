⟦p127⟧And other cases,

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p127, after Annotation LXXXVII (the 86th); it carries no marginal citations. It is the phrase *& autres cas* of the decision (texte-77), set as the head of the next annotation; the print here has the singular *Et autre cas,* with the comma of the decision's running list, and Coras's annotation takes it up in the plural (*Les autres cas*). Translated in the plural, as the decision has it.
