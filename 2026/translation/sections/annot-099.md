⟦p142⟧There was great reason to dismiss this poor Pierre Guerre from the case and suit; who, so far from deserving to be punished, was worthy of recompense, and merited double praise for a work so good, so virtuous and so charitable, as to have spent part of his property and exposed his person to great peril and danger of his life, to uncover this deed and bring so prodigious an imposture to light.

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOTAT. XCIX. on p142; the transcription numbers it 98. It expounds the words *Enſemble ledit Pierre Guerre, oncle dudit Martin* of the decision (texte-97). No marginal citations. *Ce pauure Pierre Guerre* → "this poor Pierre Guerre" — the phrase the case file §3 quotes, Coras's plain sympathy for the uncle who was discharged without reward; translated as he puts it. *Mettre hors de procez, & d'inſtance* → "dismiss from the case and suit" (texte-95); *tant ſ'en faut qu'il deuſt eſtre puni* → "so far from deserving to be punished"; *recompenſe* → "recompense"; *deſpendu* → "spent"; *mettre … en euidence* → "bring … to light" (cf. *venir en evidence* → to come to light, annot-007); *prodigieuſe impoſture* → "prodigious imposture" per the glossary.
