⟦p144⟧Before whom, on the 16th of September in the said year 1560, he confessed at full length his impudent and rash crime; nevertheless he declared that what had given him the first occasion to project his brazen and monstrous enterprise had been that, seven or eight years before, he being on his return from the camp of Picardy,

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p144. The second *Texte* of the execution record (the first stands at the end of annot-099): du Tilh's confession before the Judge of Rieux at Artigat on 16 September 1560 (case file §2). The sentence is unfinished — it continues into texte-100 (*Quelques vns … le preuoyãt pour Martin Guerre*); the print closes it with a point, and the English ends on a comma so that the continuation reads on. Reflow gap read through: *ef frontee* (*effrontee*). *Confeſſa bien au long* → "confessed at full length"; *impudent, & temeraire forfaict* → "impudent and rash crime" (*forfaict* → crime, misdeed; *temeraire* → rash, per the glossary); *proietter* → "project"; *effrontee & monſtrueuſe entreprinſe* → "brazen and monstrous enterprise"; *le camp de Picardie* → "the camp of Picardy" — the army in Picardy, in which du Tilh had served before he was first taken for Martin Guerre (case file §4), and which gives Coras the occasion for the geography of annot-101. September needs no old-style adjustment (case file §8).
