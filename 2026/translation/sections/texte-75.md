⟦p100⟧Afterwards the said de Rols is made to come, who, as soon as she had cast her eyes upon the said newcomer, all in tears, and trembling like the leaf shaken by the winds, having her face all bathed in tears, ran to embrace him, asking his pardon for the fault which, through imprudence, and overcome by the seductions, impostures and wiles of the said du Tilh, she had committed.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p100, after Annotation LXXIII (printed LXXIIII); the section carries no marginal citations. It is Bertrande's recognition of the real Martin Guerre, the passage the case file §2 quotes ("trembling like a leaf shaken by the wind, her face all bathed in tears"). *On fait apres venir* → "Afterwards… is made to come"; *ladite de Rols* → "the said de Rols" per §3; *ſoudain apres auoir ietté les yeux ſur* → "as soon as she had cast her eyes upon"; *toute eſploree* → "all in tears"; *tremblante comme la fueille agitee des vents* → "trembling like the leaf shaken by the winds"; *ayant ſa face toute baignee de larmes* → "having her face all bathed in tears"; *accourut l'embraſſer* → "ran to embrace him"; *luy demandant pardon de la faute* → "asking his pardon for the fault" (*faute*, the word Annotation LXXV then takes up: *ſi cette faute eſtoit excuſable*); *par imprudence* → "through imprudence"; *ſurmontee des ſeductions, impoſtures, & cautelles* → "overcome by the seductions, impostures and wiles" (*imposture* → imposture, *cautelles* → wiles, per the glossary).
