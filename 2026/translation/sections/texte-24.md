⟦p045⟧The said du Tilh, heard upon this bewitchment and spell, the names of the priests, and the ceremonies observed, answers in everything like the said de Rols, without failing in anything, adding, or diminishing. Proceeding to the confrontations, this du Tilh requires that the said de Rols be placed in sequestration and at liberty, to avoid subornation; which is ordered and executed.
