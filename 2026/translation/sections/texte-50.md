⟦p070⟧And to this opinion, that the prisoner was Martin Guerre, the Court had great reason to incline: not only for what has been said, but moreover because this opinion favoured the marriage, the children who are ⟦p071⟧issued of it, and the cause of the accused.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE, on p070 (with a comma for the point), after Annotation XLIX; the section carries no marginal citations of its own. The margin of p071, beside this section's last line, carries a note keyed *m* which the transcription marks an orphan: it serves the marker {m} of Annotation L, whose sentence "to condemn the innocent {m}" runs over the foot of p071 onto p072, so that the note was set on p071 and the marker fell on p072. It is listed below. The page break falls between *ſont* and *iſſuz* and is kept at the corresponding words ("are ⟦p071⟧issued"). *Ceſte ſentence* → "this opinion": *ſentence* here is the court's leaning (the *sententia* as a judge's opinion or vote, as *l'aduis & la ſentence* → "the opinion and judgment" at annot-038), not the lower judge's *sentence* → judgment of §5; Annotation L takes it up as *ceſte opinion*. *Incliner* → "to incline"; *pource que dit eſt* → "for what has been said"; *pour autant encor, que* → "but moreover because"; *fauoriſoit* → "favoured"; *iſſuz* → "issued"; *la cauſe du preuenu* → "the cause of the accused". The three *favores* — of marriage, of the children, of the accused — are the heads of Annotation L.
