⟦p067⟧This point also gave great argument for thinking that there was trickery got up against the accused, and that what the said de Rols did was by the constraint, force and reverence of the said Pierre Guerre and her mother, in whose house she lived: for although one ought not easily to presume an act to have been done through terror or fear {a2}, nevertheless, when such solicitations, importunities and threats appear, the fear is sufficiently proved {b}; considering especially the quality of those who used such intimidations, who were the stepfather and the mother {c}; and considering the place also, for it was in their house, where the said de Rols was maintained, and held in captivity {d}; and considering moreover her own qua⟦p068⟧lity, who was a woman, who is frightened and terrified at a small thing {e}, and moreover lets herself easily be persuaded — and in her, persuasions have no less force than threats or force {f}. Added to which also, she daily made complaint of it against her said stepfather and mother {g}.

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOTAT. XLVI. on p067. The marker alphabet restarts at *a* and runs a–g across p067–p068. The transcription keys the first note *a2*, since p067 carries two notes lettered *a* (the other belongs to Annotation XLV); the print has a plain *a*, and the marker is kept as the transcription keys it. The margin records notes *a*, *b*, *c*, *e*, *f* and *g*, but none keyed *d*: the transcription's note *c* runs two citations together ("l... P. quæ onerandæ. D. quar. rer." and "car. cerẽ D. quod met. cau."), and the second, on imprisonment, evidently serves the marker {d} ("held in captivity"); it is listed below at {d} with a remark. The page break falls inside *qua|lité* and is kept at the corresponding word ("qua⟦p068⟧lity"). The `⟨alt⟩` at *cõtrainte,⟨alt:cõrrainte,⟩* is taken as *cõtrainte* ("constraint"); the alternative is a wrong sort, and *cõrrainte* is no word. Wrong sorts and slips translated by sense: *doyuq* for *doyue* (*doive*); *reuerance* for *reuerence*; *efraye* for *effraye*; *ſeſd.* for *ſeſdits*; *a la maiſon* without grave; in the margin *quo ries* for *quoties* (note *a*), *l...* with the incipit lost (note *c*), *aquir.* for *acquir.* (note *g*), and an italic capital *P* for the paragraph sign § in notes *a*, *c*, *f* and *g*. Reflow gaps read through: *mai ſon*, *ver tu*, *quo ries*, *do lo*, *car cerẽ*. The print sets a point after {d} ("captiuité {d}. & attendu"), rendered by a semicolon. *Fourbe dreſſee contre* → "trickery got up against" (cf. *tromperie* → trickery); *contrainte, force, & reuerence* → "constraint, force and reverence" (the *reverentia* owed to parents, which the law counts with fear as a ground of compulsion); *à la maiſon deſquels elle ſe tenoit* → "in whose house she lived"; *terreur, ou crainte* → "terror or fear" (the *metus* of the Digest title cited at {d}); *ſollicitations, importunitez, & menaces* → "solicitations, importunities and threats"; *la qualité de ceux* → "the quality of those" (their standing); *paratre* → "stepfather"; *nourrie* → "maintained" (kept and fed, as at texte-36 *nourries*); *tenue en captiuité* → "held in captivity"; *ſ'efraye, & eſpouuante pour peu de choſe* → "is frightened and terrified at a small thing"; *& ſi ſe laiſſe facilement perſuader* → "and moreover lets herself easily be persuaded" (*& ſi*, "and moreover"); *vertu* → "force" (as *de petite vertu* → "of small force" at annot-041); *Ioinct auſſi que* → "Joined also that"; *iournellemẽt* → "daily".
