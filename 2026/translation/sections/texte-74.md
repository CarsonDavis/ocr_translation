⟦p098⟧Saying to the Commissioners: here is my brother Martin Guerre; and I confess frankly the error in which this abominable traitor (pointing to the said du Tilh, there present) had by false tokens placed me and my other sisters, nay all the people of Artigat, and long kept us. Whereupon the said newcomer began to weep also. Afterwards the other sisters likewise recognised him, and, to be brief, all the other witnesses who be⟦p099⟧fore had so firmly maintained the prisoner to be Martin Guerre.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE on p098 (without the point), after Annotation LXXII (printed LXXIII); the section carries no marginal citations. It continues texte-70 as a participial clause (*Diſant aux Commiſſaires*, "Saying to the Commissioners") and is kept so; the eldest sister's words pass from direct speech (*voicy mon frere*) into the first person without a change of construction, and the English does the same. The print opens a parenthesis at *(monſtrant ledit du Tilh, illec preſent* and never closes it; the English closes it after "there present", where the sense requires. The last sentence has no verb of its own — *tous les autres teſmoins qui auparauant auoyent ſi fermement ſouſtenu…* — the *le recognoiſſoient* of the sisters being understood; the ellipsis is kept. The page break falls inside *aupar|auant* and is kept at the corresponding word ("be⟦p099⟧fore"). Wrong sort translated by sense: *enrtetenu* for *entretenu*. *Commiſſaires* → "Commissioners", the judges delegated to examine, per the glossary (annot-021); *confeſſe frãchement* → "confess frankly"; *l'erreur, auquel… m'auoit… conſtitué & longuemẽt entretenu* → "the error in which… had… placed me… and long kept us" (*erreur* → error, the legal *error personae* of the glossary, texte-10); *proditeur* → "traitor" (new glossary entry; cf. *proditoirement* → treacherously; Coras uses the word again at texte-75 and annot-079); *par fauſſes enſeignes* → "by false tokens" (cf. *veritables enseignes* → true tokens); *voire à tout le peuple d'Artigat* → "nay all the people of Artigat"; *ſe mit à plourer* → "began to weep"; *pour faire brief* → "to be brief"; *ſi fermement ſouſtenu* → "so firmly maintained".
