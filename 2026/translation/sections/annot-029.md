⟦p051⟧Besides that it is not probable, for the proximity of blood, that the uncle should not know his own nephew {a}, or that he should be so brutal and unnatural as to wish to annihilate and destroy his own blood. It is certain also that no person (unless he be more felonious than the savage beasts) ever held his own flesh in hatred, but maintains, nourishes and preserves it, to the utmost of his power {b}. Whence Saint Paul called him worse than an infidel who has no care of his own {ſ}.

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOTAT. XXIX. on p051, correctly. The marker alphabet is defective on p051: the body runs {a} {b} {ſ}, the third marker being printed as a small superior long-s-shaped sort, while the margin note it serves (1 Timothy 5) is keyed with a clear *c* and the page's alphabet runs *a*, *b*, *c*. The sort is presumably a wrong sort standing for *c*; both marker and key are kept exactly as printed, and the note is listed below under its key with the marker it serves. The print's *conſeque* (for *conſerue*) is a wrong sort, translated by sense as "preserves".

- {a} (p051): **Digest 38.8 (*Unde cognati*), the law cited as *l. octaui* (not identified); Code, title *De in integrum restitutione* (one of the restitution titles of Code book 2; the law cited as *l. de tutela* not identified)** — l. octaui, D. vnde cog. l. de tutela. C de in integ. reſt. [Cited for the presumption that near kin know one another.]
- {b} (p051): **Ephesians 5 (5:29)** — Epheſiens. c. v [*Nemo enim umquam carnem suam odio habuit, sed nutrit et fovet eam* — for no man ever yet hated his own flesh, but nourisheth and cherisheth it.]
- {c} (p051) — the note keyed *c*, served by the body marker printed {ſ}: **1 Timothy 5 (5:8)** — La i. de Ti moth. c. v. [*Si quis autem suorum … curam non habet, fidem negavit et est infideli deterior* — if any have not care of his own, he hath denied the faith and is worse than an infidel.]
