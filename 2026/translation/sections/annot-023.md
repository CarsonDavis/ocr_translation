⟦p044⟧Incredible certainly is the ruse and wiliness of Satan, who, like a roaring lion, ever walks about around men, to catch and deceive some one of them, {a} transfiguring himself oftentimes into an Angel of light, the better to envelop and entice him {b}; nay, he so well embellishes and adorns his darkness with his colours that to many the very night and obscurity seem to be a splendour and perfect light — as in this unbewitching, in which, under the pretext of piety and of ecclesiastical ceremonies, he grafted a horrible and cruel poison, persuading these poor married people that there was no other means to unbewitch than the superstition of eating hosts and *fouaces*, thus leaving aside contrition of heart, humiliation of spirit, alms, fasts and prayers, which are the true and excellent exorcisms to present to God {c}, placing in Him all one's hope, and not in ⟦p045⟧vain superstitions and other such useless things. {d}

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOT. XXIIII. on p044; it is the twenty-third in sequence, headed here as Annotation XXIII (the next annotation, on p045, is likewise printed XXIIII, correctly). The section as segmented also carries the printer's next TEXT block (the record of du Tilh's answers and his request for sequestration), which the print heads TFXTE. — a wrong sort for TEXTE — and which is translated here in its place. The print's marker alphabet is defective on p044–p045: the body runs {a} {b} {c} {d}, but the margin carries only the notes keyed *a* and *b*; the markers *c* and *d* have no note. Markers are kept exactly as printed.

- {a} (p044): **1 Peter 5 (5:8)** — A la premiere des S. Pierre. c. v. [*Adversarius vester diabolus tamquam leo rugiens circuit, quaerens quem devoret* — your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour.]
- {b} (p044): **2 Corinthians 11 (11:14)** — A la ſecon de des Corin tans. c. xi. [*Ipse enim Satanas transfigurat se in angelum lucis* — Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light. The print's *Corintans* is as printed.]
- {c} (p044) — marker with no note in the margin.
- {d} (p045) — marker with no note in the margin.
