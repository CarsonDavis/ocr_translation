⟦p032⟧Where, although the interval of time had made some change in his face, and even though at his departure he had had no hair ⟦p033⟧of beard, nevertheless he was recognised by all, and singularly by the said Pierre Guerre his uncle, who had received and made much of him as his nephew: until, looking more closely to his affairs, the defendant wished to recover his said property, and the fruits that had been received from it during his absence; whereof the said Pierre Guerre, the uncle, having often been amicably admonished, had for a long time fed him with fine words.
