⟦p060⟧Among whom there are also three or four kinds of witnesses who are of great consideration. First, the four sisters, of whom we have spoken above: women of worth and honest, if there are any in Gascony; who have always constantly maintained that the prisoner was certainly Martin Guerre their brother, and husband of the said de Rols, and that they knew him perfectly to be such. And the like assurance was given by two of the brothers-in-law of the said Martin, married to two of the said sisters.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. at the top of p060. Wrong sorts translated by sense: *Preniierement* for *Premierement*, *Cuerre* for *Guerre*, *dõnce* for *dõnee* (*donnee*). Reflow gap read through: *parfai tement*. *Qualitez de teſmoins* → "kinds of witnesses" as at texte-28; *en grãde conſideration* → "of great consideration"; *femmes de bien & hõneſtes* → "women of worth and honest" (cf. *homme de bien* → "man of worth" at annot-025); *ſ'il en y à en la Gaſcogne* → "if there are any in Gascony" (Davis: "as good and honourable women as are to be found in Gascony"); *deſquelles nous auõs ci deuant parlé* refers to texte-36; *conſtamment ſouſtenu* → "constantly maintained"; *pareille aſſeurance ont dõnee* → "the like assurance was given by" (the French has the brothers-in-law as subject; English passive for the word order). *Beaux-freres* → "brothers-in-law".
