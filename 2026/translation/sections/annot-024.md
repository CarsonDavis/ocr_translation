⟦p045⟧We have shown above that the sequestration of goods, and still more of persons, is odious and prohibited, unless it be for some great and legitimate cause, such as is in this case the fear of seduction and subornation. {a}

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOTAT, XXIIII. on p045 (with a comma after ANNOTAT); the number is correct for the twenty-fourth in sequence, although the preceding annotation on p044 is also printed XXIIII. "Shown above" refers to Annotation XX (on the sequestration of Bertrande de Rols).
