⟦p072⟧As also because they affirm and depose in favour of the marriage, of the children, and of the accused.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p072, after Annotation LI; the section carries no marginal citations. The clause continues the sentence begun in the TEXT block carried inside Annotation L ("more faith must be given — both because they depose things more probable", *tant par ce que…*), and *Que pour autant auſsi* answers to that *tant*; it is rendered "As also because". *Affermẽt & depoſent* → "affirm and depose" (*deposer* → to depose per the glossary); *en faueur du mariage, des enfans, & du preuenu* → "in favour of the marriage, of the children, and of the accused" — the three *favores* of Annotation L.
