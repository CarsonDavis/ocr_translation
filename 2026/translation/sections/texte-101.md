⟦p142⟧And has remanded, and remands, the said du Tilh to the said Judge of Rieux, to have this present decision put into execution according to its form and tenor.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p142. The last clause of the decision; *a renuoyé, & renuoye* → "has remanded, and remands", *ce preſent arreſt* → "this present decision" (*arrest* → decision, case file §5), *ſelon ſa forme, & teneur* → "according to its form and tenor", all as at texte-77, where the same words were translated in the full text of the decision.
