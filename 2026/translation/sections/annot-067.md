⟦p086⟧One does not readily, and without cause, presume an error in any person whatsoever {a}; nevertheless, once a man is in some error, it is to be presumed that he perseveres in it, unless the contrary appears {b}: for a change of will is not to be presumed, and still less that any new accidents have supervened which could cause it. But rather, on the contrary, one presumes from the past to the present time {d}: as that an enemy, a perjurer, a rich man, a poor man, a subject, an excommunicate, is still so at this day {e}. Whence the said de Rols, circumvented by her sisters-in-law and uncle, who had so thoroughly murmured it in her ear, and so often assured her that the prisoner was truly Martin Guerre, having taken up such a persuasion, was justly given occasion to love, receive and defend him as her husband.

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOTAT. LXVII. on p086; it is the sixty-sixth in the running count (the print's numbers run one ahead of the count from Annotation LX onwards), and the transcription flags the number as uncertain. The marker alphabet restarts at *a*; the body carries {a}, {b}, {d} and {e}, all on p086, and each is served by a keyed note. The margin also carries a note keyed *c* ("l. eum qui. D. de probat. Accurſe, en la l. ſi vero…"), for which the body has no marker (the transcription marks it an orphan): by its content — the gloss on presumption — it serves the clause "for a change of will is not to be presumed, and still less that any new accidents have supervened which could cause it", where the print has a plain full stop; it is listed below as an orphan at its evident place, and the body is kept marker-free there, as the French is. The print sets a point after {a} ("quelconque {a}. toutesfois") and after {d} ("preſent {d}. comme"), rendered by a semicolon and a colon, since the sentences continue. The `⟨alt⟩` readings in the margin are taken as *l.* (note *c*; the abbreviation of *lex*, with its point), *c.* (note *e*; *capitulum*, with its point) and *Balde* (note *e*; Baldus — *Balae* is no jurist's name). Reflow gaps read through: *pro ba.* (note *d*). *Volontiers* → "readily"; *perſeuere en iceluy* → "perseveres in it"; *ſ'il n'appert du contraire* → "unless the contrary appears"; *le changement de volõté* → "a change of will" (the doctors' *mutatio voluntatis non praesumitur*); *aucuns nouueaux accidens ſuruiennent* → "any new accidents have supervened"; *on preſume du paſſé au temps preſent* → "one presumes from the past to the present time" (the presumption *de praeterito ad praesens*); *vn ſuiet* → "a subject"; *vn excõmunié* → "an excommunicate"; *pour ce iourd'huy* → "at this day"; *circonuenue* → "circumvented" per the glossary; *ſes belles ſeurs & oncle* → "her sisters-in-law and uncle" (Martin's four sisters and Pierre Guerre, her uncle by marriage and stepfather); *obmurmuré* → "murmured it in her ear" (Latin *obmurmurare*, to murmur against or at someone; here, the repeated whispering that wore her down); *ayant priſe telle perſuaſion* → "having taken up such a persuasion"; *elle iuſtement eſtoit occaſionnée de* → "was justly given occasion to"; *l'aimer receuoir & defendre* → "to love, receive and defend him" (the print has no comma after *aimer*).
