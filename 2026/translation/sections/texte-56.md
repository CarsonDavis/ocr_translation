⟦p076⟧And as to saying that the said prisoner does not know how to speak the language of the Basques, the truth of the fact supplies the answer: for it results from the inquiries that Martin Guerre was brought a little child from his native land, and that he was then only two years old, or thereabouts

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p076, after Annotation LV; the section carries no marginal citations. It answers the prisoner's point at texte-34 (that Martin Guerre, a native Basque, should know Basque, which the prisoner does not); Annotation XXXV discussed it. The French ends without a stop before the heading of Annotation LVI, and the English does likewise. The `⟨alt⟩` at *reſu[?]te.⟨alt:reſulte.⟩* is taken as B, *reſulte* ("it results"): reading A leaves a letter unread, and *resulte* is the only word the context admits; the print's stop after *reſulte.* is a slip and is not rendered. *La langue de Baſcouz* → "the language of the Basques" (*les Basques* → the Basque country, per §4 of the case file and texte-08); *la verité du faict apporte la reſponſe* → "the truth of the fact supplies the answer"; *par les enqueſtes* → "from the inquiries" (the *enquestes* of the record, per §5); *apporté petit enfant de ſa patrie* → "brought a little child from his native land" (*patrie* → native land, as at texte-16); *pour lors* → "then"; *q̃* is the abbreviation of *que* ("n'auoit … que deux ans", "was only two years old").
