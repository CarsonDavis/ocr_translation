## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p086, after Annotation LXVI (the print's LXVII); the section carries no marginal citations. The sentence is left open, as the French is: it runs on into texte-66 (*Lequel arriué des Eſpagnes…*). The page break falls inside *demeu|raſt* and is kept at the corresponding word ("re⟦p087⟧main"). Reflow is clean; the print's *aſsiſter* has a round *s* before *i*. *Au conflict de tant & diuerſes raiſons* → "In the conflict of so many and divers reasons"; *repugnance de coniectures & preuues* → "the repugnance of the conjectures and proofs" (the glossary's *preuves repugnantes* → repugnant proofs, and *conflict des conjectures* at texte-26: conflicting proofs that cannot stand together; Ringold and Lewis's "unwillingness to accept conjectures and proofs" mistakes the word); *perplexité grande* → "great perplexity" per the glossary (*perplexité*, Coras's word for the court's state); *le bon & tout-puiſſant Dieu* → "the good and almighty God"; *aſsiſter à la iuſtice* → "to assist justice"; *vn ſi prodigieux faict* → "so prodigious a deed" (*prodigieux* in its portentous sense, per the glossary); *ſur le poinct qu'on vouloit iuger le proces* → "at the point when the case was about to be judged" (*procès* → case; *iuger* of the Court's decision); *fait comme par vn miracle apparoiſtre* → "made … appear, as by a miracle". The theme of the miracle is taken up by Annotation LXVII.
