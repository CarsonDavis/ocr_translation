⟦p127⟧The other cases are several other acts of cheating, ⟦p128⟧of which this du Tilh stood convicted, besides the ordinary blasphemies which this rogue habitually used, as we have made amply to appear above, where also the penalty of blasphemers has been spoken of {a}.

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOT. LXXXVIII. on p127; by the true count it is the 87th (the print's numbers run one ahead from Annotation 84), and it expounds the words *& autres cas* of the decision (texte-86). One marker, *a*, on p128, keyed and served. The page break falls between *affrontemens;* and *deſquels* and is kept at the corresponding word. *Affrontemens* → "acts of cheating" (*affronter / affrontement* → to cheat / effrontery, cheating, per the glossary; here countable); *demeuroit conuaincu* → "stood convicted"; *blaſphemes* → "blasphemies"; *paillard* → "rogue" per the glossary (as at annot-047); *couſtumierement vſoit* → "habitually used" (cf. *couſtumiers & endurcis* → accustomed and hardened, annot-036); *fait amplement apparoir* → "made amply to appear". The cross-reference is correct: Annotation XXXVI (annot-036, p057–p058) treats du Tilh's oaths and the penalty of blasphemers.

- {a} (p128): **Coras's own Annotation XXXVI (annot-036), on blasphemy and its penalty** — En l'annotation xxxvj.
