⟦p147⟧And in order to attain it the more conveniently, he resolved to inquire and inform himself, as warily as he could, from the said Pivol, Guilher [the print here spells them *Puiol*, *Quillet*], and other familiar friends and neighbours, of the condition of the said Martin Guerre: of his father, wife, sisters, uncle and other kinsfolk; together with what the same Martin ⟦p148⟧[was wont to] say and do before going away [unclear: the French has no verb between *Martin* and *dire*].

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p147. The body carries no marker and the margin no note. The page break falls between *iceluy Martin* (the last line of p147) and *dire, & faire* (the first line of p148); the French as transcribed has no finite verb there (*de ce qu'iceluy Martin | dire, & faire auant que ſ'en aller*), so a word such as *ſouloit*, "was wont to", has dropped — whether in the print or at the page turn is for the transcribers; "[was wont to]" is supplied in brackets and the gap flagged `[unclear]`. Reflow gap read through: *pa rens*. *Pour paruenir plus commodement* → "in order to attain it the more conveniently" (the object, his design, understood from texte-101); *ſ'aduiſa de* → "he resolved to", as at texte-101; *ſ'enquerir, & informer* → "inquire and inform himself"; *le plus cautement qu'il pourroit* → "as warily as he could" (*caut* → cunning, annot-077; *cautement* here of care, not craft); *leſdits Puiol, Quillet* → "the said Pujol, Quillet" — the copy-text's forms on this page for the two men printed *Piuol* and *Guilher* on p145 (texte-100) and given by the case file §3 as Pivol and Guilhet, where the variants across the editions are noted; *i* is rendered *j* in *Puiol* as the house renders *Iean* "Jean"; *l'eſtat dud. Martin Guerre* → "the condition of the said Martin Guerre" (*eſtat*, state or condition, the same word annot-104 glosses from the jurists); *autres parens* → "other kinsfolk"; *auant que ſ'en aller* → "before going away".
