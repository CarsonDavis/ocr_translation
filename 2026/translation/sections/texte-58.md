⟦p077⟧True also that, on the other hand, it does not seem very hard to answer the reasons deduced by the accused. For as to saying, first, that more faith must be given to the witnesses who depose for the prisoner, because they affirm: this reason cannot be fitted to this case, for the other witnesses also, or the greater part, assure — namely, that the accused is Arnauld du Tilh. Added to which, the negation they make, that the prisoner is not Martin Guerre, comes easily into proof, inasmuch as they restrict themselves so well to places, times and persons that we are outside the terms of that common ru⟦p078⟧le, that two witnesses who affirm are more credible than a thousand who deny.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p077, after Annotation LVII; the section carries no marginal citations. The page break falls inside *rei|gle* and is kept at the corresponding word ("ru⟦p078⟧le"). The record here turns from answering the points against the prisoner to answering the points for him, beginning with the point at texte-49 (that the witnesses for the prisoner affirm, and depose in favour of the marriage, the children and the accused), on which Annotation LII enlarged. *Vray auſſi, que d'ailleurs* → "True also that, on the other hand"; *ne ſemble pas fort malaiſé* → "it does not seem very hard"; *raiſons deduites* → "reasons deduced" (*deduire* → to deduce, of a party's pleading, per the glossary at texte-16); *donner plus de foy aux teſmoins* → "more faith must be given to the witnesses"; *afferment* → "affirm", *aſſeurent* → "assure", *nient* → "deny", the three verbs kept distinct as in the French; *accommodée à ce faict* → "fitted to this case"; *à ſçauoir que* → "namely, that"; *ioint que* → "Joined that" (as *Ioinct auſſi que* at annot-046); *la negation qu'ils font* → "the negation they make"; *vient aiſéement en preuue* → "comes easily into proof"; *ſe reſtraignent … aux lieux, temps, & perſonnes* → "restrict themselves … to places, times and persons" (the jurists' *negativa coarctata*, which Annotation LVIII explains); *hors des termes de* → "outside the terms of"; *ceſte vulgaire reigle* → "that common rule" (cf. *vulgaires & communes reigles* at annot-020); *plus croyables* → "more credible".
