⟦p152⟧Making a particular enumeration of the whole, together with what was owed to him, and by what persons.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p152, after Annotation CVIII. (the 107th by the true count); it carries no marginal citations. It continues the confession of texte-105 (du Tilh's debts, named in his hearing before the judge of Rieux): he now itemises the whole, and adds what was owed to him and by whom. *Faiſant du tout particulier denombrement* → "Making a particular enumeration of the whole" (*denombrement*, the itemised list of a declaration); *ce que luy eſtoit déu* → "what was owed to him"; *par quelles perſonnes* → "by what persons". It is this declaration of debts and credits at the hour of death that Annotation CIX (annot-108) takes up.
