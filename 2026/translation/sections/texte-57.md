⟦p076⟧Nor is it anything to the purpose, either, that the said du Tilh ⟦p077⟧has been from his youth dissolute, of evil life, and given to every kind of wickedness: for it does not appear that the prisoner is that man, but rather Martin Guerre.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p076, after Annotation LVI; the section carries no marginal citations. The page break falls between *du Tilh* and *ay eſté* and is kept at the corresponding words ("du Tilh ⟦p077⟧has been"). It answers the prisoner's point at texte-35 (du Tilh's dissolute youth), on which Annotations XXXVI and XXXVII turn. Wrong sort translated by sense: *ay eſté* for *ait eſté*. *Ny fait rien auſsi, que* → "Nor is it anything to the purpose, either, that" (the record's formula *n'y fait rien*, "is nothing to the matter", as at texte-50 and texte-51); *diſſolu, de mauuaiſe vie* → "dissolute, of evil life"; *adonné à toute eſpece de meſchancetez* → "given to every kind of wickedness" (*meschanceté* → wickedness, per the glossary); *il n'appert point* → "it does not appear"; *celuy la* → "that man"; *ains plus toſt* → "but rather".
