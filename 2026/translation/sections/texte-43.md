⟦p064⟧Now such things cannot fall within an instruction given him by another; ⟦p065⟧for one may well teach certain speeches, and give tokens and marks: but to impart the knowledge of so many and so many divers persons, never seen or known — that is impossible, otherwise than by Magic or some reprobate art. And so much for the matter of the proof by witnesses. In the second place, a summary inquiry having been made upon the likeness of the accused with the sisters of Martin Guerre, it is reported — and better still by several witnesses heard in the inquiries on the court's own motion — that eggs are not more alike among themselves.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p064, after Annotation XLII; the section carries no marginal citations. The page break falls between *autre* and *car* and is kept there ("by another; ⟦p065⟧for"). Wrong sorts and slips translated by sense: *congoiſſance* for *cognoiſſance*; *par* for *pas* ("ne ſont par entre eux plus ſemblables"); *donné* for *donnée*. Reflow gaps read through: *pro pos*, *Mar tin*. *Tomber en inſtruction* → "fall within an instruction" (the coaching the accused might have received); *enſeignes, & marques* → "tokens and marks" per the glossary; *bailler la congoiſſance de* → "to impart the knowledge of"; *art reprouué* → "reprobate art" (the *artes reprobatae*, forbidden arts); *Et voila pour le faict de la preuue par teſmoins* → "And so much for the matter of the proof by witnesses"; *ſommaire appriſe* → "summary inquiry" (glossed at texte-25, where the two *sommaires aprises* are first mentioned); *enqueſtes d'office* → "inquiries on the court's own motion" per §5; *les œufs ne ſont pas entre eux plus ſemblables* → "eggs are not more alike among themselves", the proverb Annotation XLIII takes up.
