## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p101, after Annotation LXXIV (printed LXXV); the section carries no marginal citations. It is a participial continuation of Bertrande's plea in texte-72 (*Accuſant les ſeurs…*, "Accusing the sisters…") and ends on a comma in the print, the sentence continuing in texte-74 (*Ioinct…*); both are kept. The page break falls inside *faci|lement* and is kept at the corresponding word ("eas⟦p102⟧ily"). *Les ſeurs dudit Martin* → "the sisters of the said Martin"; *ſur tous les autres* → "above all the others"; *trop facilement creu, & aſſeuré* → "too easily believed and assured" (*aſſeuré*, of a witness's positive assertion, as at texte-69 *affermé*).
