⟦p066⟧Fourthly, during the three or four years that the accused and the said de Rols were together, she never complained of it; but on the contrary, when anyone said that the prisoner was not her husband, she roughly gave him the lie, assuring that it was Martin Guerre her husband, or some dev⟦p067⟧il in his skin; and that she had known him well; and that if anyone were henceforth so mad as to say the contrary, she would have him put to death.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p066, after Annotation XLIIII; the section carries no marginal citations. The page break falls inside *dia|ble* and is kept at the corresponding word ("dev⟦p067⟧il"). Reflow gap read through: *co gnu* (*cognu*). *Ne ſ'en eſt oncques plainte* → "never complained of it"; *ains au contraire* → "but on the contrary"; *elle le deſmentoit rudement* → "she roughly gave him the lie" (*desmentir*, to give the lie to); *quelque diable en ſa peau* → "some devil in his skin"; *ſi fol de dire* → "so mad as to say"; *elle le feroit mourir* → "she would have him put to death" (the phrase used of the plot at texte-39, *faire mourir*). This is the fourth of the prisoner's points, taken up in Annotation XLV.
