⟦p124⟧Sacrilege.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p124, after Annotation LXXXIII; it carries no marginal citations. It is the single word *Sacrilege* of the decision (texte-77: *impoſture, fauſſeté, ſuppoſition de nom, & perſonne, adultere, rapt, ſacrilege, plage, larrecin, & autres cas*), set as the head of the next annotation in Coras's exposition of the words of the decision. *Sacrilege* → "Sacrilege" per the glossary (here the profanation of the sacrament of marriage, which is how Annotation 84 reads it).
