⟦p069⟧And nevertheless, on the morrow, early in the morning, the said Pierre Guerre, joint attorney of the said de Rols, accompanied by his sons-in-law, all armed, had him made prisoner, although at that time there could be no new charges, and the said de Rols had not yet made the said Pierre Guerre her attorney to those ends: for the procuration [the power of attorney] was not made that day until the evening after vespers, as this same Pierre Guerre has himself since confessed.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p069, after Annotation XLVII; the section carries no marginal citations. Reflow gap read through: *Pier re* (*Pierre*). *Le lendemain de grand matin* → "on the morrow, early in the morning"; *comprocureur* → "joint attorney" (*procureur* of a private party → attorney per the glossary; the *com-* marks him as acting jointly with, or on behalf of, the civil party); *beaux fils* → "sons-in-law" per the glossary; *le fit conſtituer priſonnier* → "had him made prisoner" (as *conſtitué priſonnier* → "made prisoner" at texte-45); *ni peuſt auoir nouuelles charges* (= *n'y peuſt*) → "there could be no new charges"; *fait procureur … à ces fins* → "made … her attorney to those ends"; *la procure* → "the procuration [the power of attorney]", glossed in brackets on this first use (Annotation XLVIII has the full form *procuration*); *apres veſpres* → "after vespers"; *iceluy Pierre Guerre meſme depuis a confeſſé* → "this same Pierre Guerre has himself since confessed". This is the arrest the morning after the night related in texte-45; the case file §2 records it, and the point that the procuration was made only that evening.
