⟦p085⟧Added to which, there are persons who are so superstitious that they would not dare to swear, even were it for things evidently true.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p085; the page carries no marginal citations. This clause resumes the sentence broken off at texte-62. *Ioinct que* → "Added to which" (the record's formula for a further ground; cf. *Ioinct* elsewhere in the *Texte*); *ſi ſuperſtitieuſes* → "so superstitious"; the print's *qu'ils* after the feminine *perſonnes* is translated by "they"; *n'oſeroyent iurer* → "would not dare to swear"; *& fuſt-il pour choſes euidemment veritables* → "even were it for things evidently true".
