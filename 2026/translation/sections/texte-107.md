⟦p150⟧For the rest, he confesses to having been a very bad scapegrace in all ways: even to having committed several thefts and cheats.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p150. The body carries no marker and the margin no note. One sentence, the subject (du Tilh in his confession before the judge of Rieux) understood, as the record's elliptical style has it. *Au reſte* → "For the rest"; *fort mauuais garnimen en toutes ſortes* → "a very bad scapegrace in all ways" (*garnement*, a worthless fellow; the print's *garnimen* is a wrong sort); *pluſieurs larrecins, & affrontemens* → "several thefts and cheats" (*larrecins & affrontemens* → theft and cheating, texte-35; *affronter / affrontement* → to cheat / cheating per the glossary). The same two counts appear in the character evidence against him at texte-35; annot-106 draws the inference.
