⟦p089⟧Here is a case most strange and very marvellous: that a wicked man, a cheat and an abominable impostor, having assumed by substitution the name and person of another, should be firmer, more constant and more truthful in rendering a reason for things than that very man with whose name and person he had clothed himself.

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOTAT. LXX. on p089; it is the sixty-ninth in the running count (the print's numbers run one ahead of the count from Annotation LX onwards), and the transcription flags the number as uncertain. It carries no marginal citations and no markers. Reflow is clean. *Voici vn cas bien eſtrange & fort eſmerueillable* → "Here is a case most strange and very marvellous" (*esmerveillable* → marvellous per the glossary); *meſchant, affronteur, & impoſteur abominable* → "a wicked man, a cheat and an abominable impostor" (per the glossary); *ayant ſuppoſé le nom & la perſonne d'vn autre* → "having assumed by substitution the name and person of another" (*supposer son nom* → to assume by substitution, texte-15; *supposition de nom & personne*, the count in the decision); *ferme, conſtant, & veritable* → "firm, constant and truthful"; *à rendre raiſon des choſes* → "in rendering a reason for things" (*rendre raison de son dire*, the witness's grounds, annot-028); *celuy-la meſmes* → "that very man"; *du nom, & de la perſonne duquel il ſ'eſtoit reueſtu* → "with whose name and person he had clothed himself" (*se revestir de*, to put on as a garment).
