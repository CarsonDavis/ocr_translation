⟦p081⟧And as to the other marks, such as the supernumerary teeth and the like: it ⟦p082⟧is no new thing that two persons should resemble each other, not only in the features and lineaments of the face, but also in certain particular signs of the body.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p081; the page carries no marginal citations. The page break falls between *co* and *n'eſt* in the print, where *co* is a wrong sort for *ce* (*ce n'eſt pas choſe nouuelle*); the marker is kept at the corresponding word ("it ⟦p082⟧is"). *Soubre-dents* (modern *surdents*) → "supernumerary teeth" — teeth growing over or beside the others, one of the marks of identification recited earlier in the record; *ſe rapportent* → "resemble each other"; *traicts, & lineamens du viſaige* → "features and lineaments of the face" (as at annot-005); *ſignes particuliers du corps* → "particular signs of the body"; *marques* → "marks" per the glossary.
