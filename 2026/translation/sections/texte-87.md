⟦p125⟧Enslavement [*plaige*: the Roman *plagium*, the stealing of a free person].

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p125, after Annotation LXXXV (the 84th); it carries no marginal citations. It is the single word *Plaige* of the decision (texte-77 prints it *plage*), set as the head of the next annotation. *Plaige* → "Enslavement" per the glossary (*plage / plagiat* → enslavement, the Roman crime of *plagium*, the stealing or selling of a free person; never "plagiarism"), with the French and the Latin in brackets on this first use as a word of the decision, since the English does not of itself recall the *lex Fabia* the next annotation cites.
