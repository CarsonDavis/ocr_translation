⟦p072⟧These three points were of great consideration for the accused: for in the first place, much more faith is given to two witnesses who affirm some fact than to a thousand others {a2}. And next, in things which admit of some doubt, the favour either of the marriage, or of the children, or of the accused makes the balance fall (as was said a little before) to that side {b2}: with stronger reason, ⟦p073⟧then, when all these favours present themselves together {c}.

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOT. LII. on p072, in the shortened form; the number is right. The marker alphabet restarts at *a* and runs a–c across p072–p073; the transcription keys the first two markers and notes *a2* and *b2*, since p072 already carries Annotation LI's notes *a* and *b*, and they are kept as the transcription has them (the print has plain *a* and *b*). The third marker is an `⟨alt⟩`: *{e}.⟨alt:{c}.⟩*, the print's sort read as *e* or *c*. Reading B, {c}, is taken: the alphabet runs a, b and then c; the margin of p072 carries a note keyed *c* (*Aud. itaque. C. con. de ſuc.*) which the transcription marks an orphan, having no marker to serve, and which belongs to this marker; and the note keyed *c* that the transcription attaches to this annotation on p073 (*c. tam literis. c. licet ex quadam. de teſtib. c. tua. de conſang. & affinit.*) is by its content — hearsay — the note for Annotation LIII's marker {c} ("not sufficient to make proof"), which that annotation's margin otherwise lacks; it is listed below with that remark, and translated at annot-053 {c}. The body is written with the chosen marker {c} only; the checker, which reads both readings of the alt from the French, therefore reports the body's markers as differing from the French until the choice is applied (see the batch report). The page break falls between *raiſon* and *doncques* and is kept at the corresponding words ("with stronger reason, ⟦p073⟧then"). The print sets a point after {c}; kept. *Trois poincts* → "three points"; *en grande conſideration pour* → "of great consideration for"; *on donne beaucoup plus de foy à deux teſmoins qui aſſeurent quelque faict, qu'à mille autres* → "much more faith is given to two witnesses who affirm some fact than to a thousand others" (the rule the record turns against the prisoner at texte-55 and Annotation LVIII); *la faueur, ou du mariage ou des enfans, ou du preuenu* → "the favour either of the marriage, or of the children, or of the accused"; *font tomber la balance … de ce coſté* → "makes the balance fall … to that side"; *comme peu deuant a eſté dit* → "as was said a little before" (Annotation L); *plus forte raiſon doncques* → "with stronger reason, then" (*a fortiori*); *ſe preſentent enſemble* → "present themselves together".
