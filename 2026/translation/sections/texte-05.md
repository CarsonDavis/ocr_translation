⟦p011⟧The said du Tilh, trusting in the fact that he entirely bore the features and lineaments of the face of the said Martin, violating in the first place all laws of friendship, and afterwards using a new kind of effrontery and trickery, had presented himself to the four sisters, uncle and relatives of this Martin, and to the said Bertrande de Rols, indeed to all those of the place of Artigat: giving to all several particular and such close tokens, that not only the strangers, but also all the said relatives, indeed the supplicant, persuaded themselves that it was truly Martin Guerre.
