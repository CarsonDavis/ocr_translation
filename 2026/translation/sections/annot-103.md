⟦p146⟧Here is a very strange case: that the most private and particular friends that Martin Guerre had had in his young age should have been placed in such error that they took the said du Tilh for the same Guerre. True it is that the best of it is more astonishing still — to hear that the near kinsfolk, even the four sisters, were in the like error; and more prodigious still, and almost incredible, that the said Bertrande de Rols, wife of the said Martin Guerre, having lived and conversed ten or twelve years with the same Martin Guerre her husband, should have had a like blindfold before her eyes.

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOTAT. CIII. on p146; the transcription numbers it 102 (the printed numbering runs one ahead, flagged uncertain). The body carries no marker and the margin no note. One sentence in the French, divided once in the English at *vray que*, with no clause dropped. *Les plus priuez & peculiers amis* → "the most private and particular friends" (*priuez & particulieres enseignes* → "private and particular tokens", texte-07; *peculier* is the Latin *peculiaris*, one's own); *en ſon ieune aage* → "in his young age"; *conſtituez en tel erreur* → "placed in such error" (*constituer… en erreur* → to place in error, texte-71; *erreur* → error, the legal *error personae*, texte-10); *vray que la meilleure eſt encore plus admirable* → "true it is that the best of it is more astonishing still" (*la meilleure*, the best part of the matter; *admirable* → astonishing per the glossary, annot-005); *prochains parẽs* → "near kinsfolk" (*parens* → kinsfolk, texte-16); *prodigieuſe* → "prodigious" per the glossary; *veſcu & conuerſé* → "lived and conversed" (*conuerſer*, of living together, as at annot-074); *vn ſemblable bandeau deuant ſes yeux* → "a like blindfold before her eyes". The "ten or twelve years" are Coras's figure for the cohabitation of Martin and Bertrande before his departure; cf. the timeline in the case file §2.
