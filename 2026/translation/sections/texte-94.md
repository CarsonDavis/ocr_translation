⟦p129⟧To be hanged and strangled.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p129; it is the words *eſtre pendu, & eſtranglé* from the *arrest* (texte-77) and carries no marginal citations. *Pendu & eſtranglé* → "hanged and strangled" per the glossary — one act, not split (§9): the strangling by the rope was the death.
