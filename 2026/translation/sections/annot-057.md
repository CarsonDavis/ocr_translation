⟦p077⟧In criminal matters, before the judge can come to condemnation, two things principally must appear to him. The first, that the crime in question has truly been committed and perpetrated {a}. The second, that the person whom one wishes to punish is the one who committed the offence, or is otherwise culpable of the deed.

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOTAT. LVII. on p077. The marker alphabet restarts at *a*: the body carries one marker, {a}; the margin carries two notes, *a* and *b*, and *b* has no marker in the body (the transcription marks it an orphan). By its content note *b* serves the second point, the clause "that the person whom one wishes to punish is the one who committed the offence", and it is listed below as an orphan. The French ends at *coulpable du faict* without a stop (the margin's key letter and the point were probably lost with it), and Annotation LVII is thus a bare statement of the two heads of proof, to which the record's next paragraph turns; the English closes the sentence with a full stop. Wrong sort translated by sense: *la perſonne qui veut punir* for *qu'on veut punir* ("the person whom one wishes to punish"), the only reading the sense admits. *Es matieres criminelles* → "In criminal matters" (*ès* = *en les*); *venir à condemnation* → "come to condemnation"; *faut qu'il luy apparoiſſe de deux choſes* → "two things must appear to him" (the judge's *constare*); *le crime duquel ſ'agiſt* → "the crime in question"; *commis & perpetré* → "committed and perpetrated"; *delict* → "offence" (*delictum*); *coulpable du faict* → "culpable of the deed". The two heads are the jurists' *corpus delicti* and the author of it.

- {a} (p077): **Digest 29.5 (*De senatus consulto Silaniano et Claudiano: quorum testamenta ne aperiantur*), l. 1, § *Item illud* (D. 29.5.1, Ulpian; paragraph not identified); Code 3.26 (*Ubi causae fiscales vel divinae domus hominumque eius agantur*), l. 1 (C. 3.26.1)** — l. j. P. item illud. D. ad Silania l. j. C. vbi cauſ. fiſcæ. [Under the Silanian decree the inquiry into the household proceeds only when it is first established that the master was killed, which is Coras's first head: that the crime was in fact committed. The bearing of the Code text could not be verified. The *P.* stands for the paragraph sign.]
- {b} (p077) — orphan note, printed in the margin with the key *b* but with no marker in the body; by its content it serves the second point, "that the person whom one wishes to punish is the one who committed the offence": **Code 9.47 (*De poenis*), l. *Sancimus* (C. 9.47.22, Arcadius and Honorius)** — l. ſancimus. C. de pœn. [*Sancimus ibi esse poenam, ubi et noxa est*: "We ordain that the punishment be there where the guilt is"; kinsmen, acquaintances and familiars are not to be touched by it.]
