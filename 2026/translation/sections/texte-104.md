⟦p146⟧Seeing which, and considering that, since the most private and particular friends of the said Martin Guerre were deceived in him, he might well, with some help, deceive and circumvent many others: he resolved to play the tragedy which you have heard above.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p146. The body carries no marker and the margin no note. The subject is still du Tilh in his confession before the judge of Rieux (texte-99, texte-100). *Les plus priuez, & peculiers amis* → "the most private and particular friends", as at annot-102; *deceuz en luy* → "deceived in him"; *auec quelque aide* → "with some help"; *deceuoir, & circonuenir* → "deceive and circumvent" (*circonvenir* → to circumvent, per the glossary); *ſ'aduiſa de* → "he resolved to" (*s'aviser*, to take it into one's mind); *iouër la tragedie* → "play the tragedy" — *tragedie* kept, per the case file §10, as one of Coras's hot words; annot-103 takes it up; *que auez ci deuant entendue* → "which you have heard above".
