⟦p072⟧One must not look so much to the number and multitude of the witnesses as to the verisimilitude of what they depose {a}; so that more faith must be given to those who depose probable things than to the others, even though these be in much greater number {b}.

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOT. LI. on p072, in the shortened form; the number is right. The marker alphabet restarts at *a* and runs a–b; both notes are keyed and both markers served. The `⟨alt⟩` in note *a* (*c⟨alt:c.⟩ & in noſtra*) is a point after the abbreviation *c.* (*capitulum*), present or absent; the two readings translate the same and neither can be preferred without the page image. Wrong sort translated by sense: in the margin *carnem* for *carmen* (note *b*: the law *Ob carmen famosum*). Reflow gap read through: *cau ſam*. *Numeroſité & multitude* → "number and multitude" (*numerosité* → multitude per the glossary, annot-021; the pair is kept as two words); *veriſimilitude* → "verisimilitude" per the glossary; *donner plus de foy* → "to give more faith"; *choſes vray-ſemblables* → "probable things" (*vray-ſemblable* → probable per the glossary); *en beaucoup plus grand nombre* → "in much greater number". The rule is the *non numeranda sed ponderanda* of the Roman and canon texts cited; Coras returns to it at annot-052 and, from the other side, at the record's reply (texte-55, annot-058, on two affirming witnesses against a thousand denying).

- {a} (p072): **Decretals X 2.19 (*De probationibus*), c. *Licet causam* (cf. X 2.19.9, Innocent III; number unverified); X 2.20 (*De testibus et attestationibus*), c. *In nostra* (chapter not identified)** — c. licet cau ſam. de prob. c⟨alt:c.⟩ & in noſtra de teſtib. [Witnesses are to be weighed by the probability of their depositions, not counted.]
- {b} (p072): **Digest 22.5 (*De testibus*), l. *Ob carmen* (D. 22.5.21, Arcadius Charisius), the last paragraph (D. 22.5.21.3)** — l. ob carnem. P fina. D. de teſtib. [*Non ad multitudinem respici oportet, sed ad sinceram testimoniorum fidem* — where witnesses differ, the judge is to regard not their number but the sincere faith of their testimony, and the evidence that most nearly agrees with the truth. The print's *carnem* is a wrong sort for *carmen*.]
