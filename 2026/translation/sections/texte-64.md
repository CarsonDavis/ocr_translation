⟦p084⟧It is easy also to answer the point that the said de Rols, confronted with the accused, refused to swear; for that can change nothing of the truth.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p084; the page carries no marginal citations. The `⟨alt⟩` at *iurer,* / *iurer',.* is resolved for reading A (*refuſa iurer, car*): the alternative reading is the same word with a stray apostrophe and a doubled stop, which carry no sense, and the comma alone suits the clause. *Il eſt aiſé auſsi de reſpondre, à ce que* → "It is easy also to answer the point that"; *confrontee au preuenu* → "confronted with the accused" (*confrontation* and *prevenu* per the procedure glossary); *refuſa iurer* → "refused to swear"; *cela ne peut changer rien de la verité* → "that can change nothing of the truth". The refusal of the oath at the confrontation is the matter of Annotations LXIII–LXV.
