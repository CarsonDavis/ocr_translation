⟦p054⟧Although this proof was not very necessary, because a testimony of having heard say [hearsay] proves nothing {a2}; nevertheless when the true Martin Guerre arri⟦p055⟧ved in such equipage, namely having a wooden leg, and bearing an attestation of having lost the foot from a cannon-shot before Saint-Quentin, the Judges began to be spurred by a sharp goad to enter into some suspicion of the imposture.

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOT. XXXIII. on p054, in the shortened form ANNOT. where the other heads have ANNOTAT.; the number is right. Its single note is a plain *a* in the print, the alphabet restarting with this annotation; since p054 already carries Annotation XXXII's note *a*, the French keys this one a2, and the marker is given so here. Wrong sorts translated by sense: *cuy dire* for *ouy dire* (hearsay); *d'eſt* for *d'eſtre* ("to be spurred"); *En cor* set as two words. The page break falls inside *arri|ua* and is kept inside "arri|ved". *Atteſtatoire* → "attestation", the certificate of his wound (cf. the glossary's *certificatoire*); *eſpoinçonnez d'vn fort eſguillon* → "spurred by a sharp goad", as in annot-014 and annot-016. Note that Coras writes *le pied* here where the *Texte* has the leg.

- {a2} (p054): **Decretals, X 2.20 (*De testibus et attestationibus*), c. *Iam litteris* (as printed; chapter not identified) and c. *Licet ex quadam* (cf. X 2.20.47, Innocent III, on witnesses who depose from hearsay; number unverified); X 4.14 (*De consanguinitate et affinitate*), c. *Tutela* (the print's *rutela*; chapter number unverified)** — c. iam literis. c, licet ex quadam, deſti. c. rutela. de. conſan. & affini, [*Deſti.* is the print's contraction for *de teſti[bus]*; *rutela* is a wrong sort for *tutela*. Cited for the rule that testimony *de auditu* makes no proof.]
