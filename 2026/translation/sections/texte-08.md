⟦p022⟧This done, the said du Tilh had first taken possession of the person of the supplicant, using her familiarly in all things, for the space of three years, as his wife; and afterwards of all the property of the said Martin, both that at Artigat and other property that the said Martin had at Hendaye, in the Basque country, of which this same Martin was a native.
