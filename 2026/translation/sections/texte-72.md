⟦p096⟧Whereupon the Court, the better to assure itself, orders that the principal witnesses who had affirmed the prisoner to be Martin Guerre should come in person, and particularly the four sisters and brothers-in-law of the said Martin, together with the uncle, ⟦p097⟧brothers and certain kinsfolk of the said Tilh, so that the two men might be exhibited to them severally and together, and they might choose from them the one whom they recognised to be truly Martin Guerre. All the said witnesses come, save the brothers of the said du Tilh, who, by multiplication of penalties, letters and commands, could not be forced to come and depose against their brother.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p096, after Annotation LXX (the print's LXVI, a misprint); the section carries no marginal citations. The page break falls between *l'oncle,* and *freres* and is kept at the corresponding words ("the uncle, ⟦p097⟧brothers"). The `⟨alt⟩` at *auoient⟨alt:auoienr⟩* is taken as *auoient* ("had"); *auoienr* is a turned sort. The print has *dudit Tilh* without *du*, kept ("the said Tilh"), and *beauxfreres* as one word; a full stop after *perſonne* where the sentence continues, rendered by a comma. Reflow gaps read through: none. *Dont* → "Whereupon"; *pour mieux ſ'aſſeurer* → "the better to assure itself"; *affermé* → "affirmed"; *viendroient en perſonne* → "should come in person"; *meſmement* → "and particularly" (in its sixteenth-century sense of "especially"); *les quatre ſeurs, & beauxfreres* → "the four sisters and brothers-in-law" (*beaux-freres* → brothers-in-law, texte-37; the case file §3 on the four sisters); *l'oncle* → "the uncle" (Carbon Barrau, du Tilh's maternal uncle, §3); *certains parẽs* → "certain kinsfolk" (*parens* → kinsfolk, texte-16); *pour leur eſtre reſpectiuement & enſemblément exhibez* → "so that the two men might be exhibited to them severally and together" — the French has no expressed subject for *exhibez*, and "the two men" (the prisoner and the newcomer) is supplied from the sense; *exhibition* → exhibition per the glossary at texte-29, the presenting of a prisoner to a witness; *choiſir d'iceux celuy* → "choose from them the one"; *reſeruez les freres* → "save the brothers"; *par multiplication de peines, lettres & cõmandemens* → "by multiplication of penalties, letters and commands" (the repeated process of the court to compel their attendance); *ne peurent eſtre forcez à venir depoſer* → "could not be forced to come and depose" (*deposer* → to depose per the glossary). Annotation LXXI excuses the brothers' refusal.
