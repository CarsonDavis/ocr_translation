⟦p049⟧From which it is easy to gather and understand that the judges were in great perplexity, seeing the state and the peril of the cause, for the conflict of the conjectures and contradiction of the proofs. For on the one side, that it was not Martin Guerre, but rather Arnauld du Tilh, or some other notorious impostor, there were five or six reasons and great conjectures. The first, a great number of witnesses, up to forty-five and more, who assured that the accused was Arnauld du Tilh, or else was not Martin Guerre.
