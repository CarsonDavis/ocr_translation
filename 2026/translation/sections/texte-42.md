⟦p063⟧Fourthly, almost all the witnesses who are heard assure that the prisoner, when he arrived at Artigat, greeted by their names all those he met of the acquaintance of Martin Guerre, without otherwise having ever seen or known them; and if they made some difficulty about recognising him, he reminded them of all things past, and said to each one particularly: Do you not remember when we were at such a place, ten, twelve, fifteen or twenty years ago, that we did such and such a thing in the presence of such and such a one, where we held such and such talk? Even to the said de Rols, his pretended wife, he discoursed, as has been shown above, the most private and particular acts that can pass between husband and wife; and at the first meeting said to her, Go and fetch me the white hose, lined with white taffeta, which I left in such a chest when I went away: which was ⟦p064⟧agreed by the said de Rols to be true, and since verified, that the hose were still there.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p063, after Annotation XLI; the section carries no marginal citations. The page break falls between *fut* and *accordé* and is kept at the corresponding words ("which was ⟦p064⟧agreed"). Reflow gaps read through: *ren controit* (*rencontroit*), *di ſoit* (*diſoit*), *tinſ mes* (*tinſmes*, "we held"). *Vamoy querir* is *Va moy querir*, "go fetch me". *Saluer de leur nom* → "greeted by their names"; *de la cognoiſſance de Martin Guerre* → "of the acquaintance of Martin Guerre"; *ramenteuoir* → "to remind"; *il a dix douze, quinze, ou vingt ans* → "ten, twelve, fifteen or twenty years ago"; *tel, & tel propos* → "such and such talk"; *ſa pretendue femme* → "his pretended wife" (*pretendu* in the older sense of "claimed", as at texte-21); *comme a eſté deſſus remonſtré* refers to texte-07, where the private tokens given to Bertrande were first mentioned; *les plus priuez & particuliers actes* → "the most private and particular acts" (cf. *privées & particulieres enseignes* at texte-07); *chauſſes blanches, doubles de taffetas blanc* → "white hose, lined with white taffeta" (*doublé*, lined; the episode the case file §2 records under the summer of 1556); *accordé … eſtre vray* → "agreed … to be true". The witnesses' report is given in the present tense as in the French (*aſſeurent*).
