⟦p078⟧And for a second, which is the principal ⟦p079⟧point of this case, the witnesses who so obstinately assured that the accused was Martin Guerre have since recognised their error, and have departed from it before the Court, as will hereafter be said.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p078, after Annotation LVIII; the section carries no marginal citations. The page break falls between *principal* and *point* and is kept at the corresponding words ("principal ⟦p079⟧point"). The `⟨alt⟩` at *depattis⟨alt:departis⟩* is taken as B, *departis* ("departed"), *depattis* being a wrong sort (*r* read as *t*); *ſe departir de* is the record's word for a witness's retraction, which Annotation LIX discusses under that name. Wrong sort translated by sense: *point ce ce faict* for *de ce faict*. *Pour vn ſecond* → "for a second" (the second answer to the prisoner's reasons, after the first at texte-55); *principal point* → "the principal point"; *ſi obſtineement aſſeuroyent* → "so obstinately assured" (cf. *opiniastrement* → obstinately); *ont depuis recognu leur erreur* → "have since recognised their error"; *ſ'en ſont departis à la court* → "have departed from it before the court" (the retraction before the Parlement, after the real Martin Guerre's arrival, which the record relates below); *comme ſera ci apres dit* → "as will hereafter be said".
