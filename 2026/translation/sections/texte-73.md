⟦p097⟧The eldest sister arrives first, who, after having contemplated the newcomer a little while, recognises him for her brother, and weeping goes to embrace him.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p097, after Annotation LXXI; the section carries no marginal citations. It is a single sentence of the record, resuming the exhibition ordered in texte-69: the witnesses are shown the two men together and asked to choose. *La ſœur aiſnee* → "the eldest sister" (the eldest of Martin's four sisters, per the case file §3); *le nouueau venu* → "the newcomer", the record's name for the real Martin Guerre since texte-66 (*nouveau venu*); *quelque peu contemplé* → "contemplated a little while"; *le recognoiſt pour ſon frere* → "recognises him for her brother"; *en pleurant le va embraſſer* → "weeping goes to embrace him". The historic presents of the French (*arriue*, *recognoiſt*, *va*) are kept.
