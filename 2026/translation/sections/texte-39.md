⟦p062⟧Moreover, several witnesses disclose the conspiracy made by the said Pierre Guerre, his wife and sons-in-law, to have the prisoner put to death and destroyed; even to having bargained with Jean Loze, consul of Palhé [Pailhès], whether he would furnish a certain sum of money on his part, and Pierre Guerre would defray the rest, to have the prisoner put to death: which the said Loze refused, saying that he would sooner give money to save him, for he was his kinsman, as Pierre Guerre himself had several times told and assured him. Moreover, they depose that the rumour at Artigat is that Pierre Guerre and his sons-in-law are making this prosecution against the will of the said de Rols; and that some of them have often heard the said Pierre Guerre say that the said prisoner was truly Martin Guerre, his nephew.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p062, after Annotation XL; the page carries no marginal citations. Wrong sorts translated by sense: *teſmoius* for *teſmoins*, *conful* for *conſul*. The print's *ſes femme & beaux fils* has a plural article governing both nouns ("his wife and sons-in-law"); its period after *priſonnier* ("le priſonnier. iuſqu'à", "le priſonnier. ce que") is rendered by a semicolon and a colon, since the sentence continues; *ladit.* and *aud.* are the print's abbreviations of *ladite* and *audit*; *que il* is printed as two words. Reflow gap read through: *mou rir*. *Cõiuration* → "conspiracy"; *faire mourir & perdre* → "to have put to death and destroyed" (cf. *ruyner & perdre* → "to ruin and destroy" at texte-18); *marchandé auec* → "bargained with"; *conful de Palhé* → "consul of Palhé [Pailhès]", the modern name per the case file §4 (Pailhès is about 4 km from Artigat; a consul was an elected municipal officer); *fournir … de ſa part* → "furnish … on his part"; *frayeroit le reſte* → "would defray the rest"; *bailleroit* → "would give"; *parẽt* → "kinsman"; *beaux fils* and *gendres* are both "sons-in-law" per the glossary; *le bruit eſt à Artigat* → "the rumour at Artigat is" (*bruit* → rumour per the glossary; the ground of Annotation XLI); *pourſuite* → "prosecution"; *nepueu* → "nephew". Jean Loze's refusal and Pierre Guerre's admission are the facts the case file §3 records under his name.
