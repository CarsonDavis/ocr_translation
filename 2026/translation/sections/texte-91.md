⟦p128⟧Of the place of Artigat.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p128, after Annotation LXXXIX (the 88th); it carries no marginal citations. It is the phrase *du lieu d'Artigat* of the decision (texte-77: *l'Egliſe du lieu d'Artigat*), set as the head of the next annotation (which explains why the *amende honorable* was made at Artigat and not at the bar of the Court). *Artigat* kept per the case file §4.
