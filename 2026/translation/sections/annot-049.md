⟦p070⟧It was not then a sign that the said de Rols wished at that time to be the party against the said accused, nor that she thought him other than Martin Guerre, since she succoured him so obligingly.

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOTAT. XLIX. on p070, correctly. The body carries no marker and the margin no note. *Faire partie à* → "to be the party against" per the glossary (*faire la partie à*, annot-039); *pour lors* → "at that time"; *le penſaſt autre que* → "thought him other than"; *le ſecouroit ſi officieuſement* → "succoured him so obligingly" (*officieuſement*, with good offices, answering to the *offices* of texte-47).
