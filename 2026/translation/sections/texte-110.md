⟦p156⟧Afterwards he institutes as his heiress his daughter Bernarde du Tilh, whom he had had of the said Bertrande de Rols; and gives her as guardians Jean du Tilh his brother, inhabitant of Le Pin, and Dominique Rebendaire, inhabitant of Toulouse.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p156, after Annotation CIX. (the 108th by the true count); it carries no marginal citations. It continues du Tilh's dispositions before the judge of Rieux on 16 September 1560 (texte-105, texte-106): the institution of his daughter as heiress and the naming of her guardians, which Annotation CX (annot-109) examines. Reflow gap read through: *Ber narde* (*Bernarde*). *Inſtitue ſon heritiere* → "institutes as his heiress" (the civil-law *institutio heredis*; *heritiere* → heiress per the glossary); *qu'il auoit euë de* → "whom he had had of"; *tuteurs* → "guardians" per the glossary; *habitant du Pin* → "inhabitant of Le Pin" (Coras's *du Pin*, per the case file §4); *Toloſe* → "Toulouse" in running prose. The persons are those of the case file §3: Bernarde du Tilh, Jean du Tilh (Arnaud's brother, of Le Pin) and Dominique Rebendaire of Toulouse.
