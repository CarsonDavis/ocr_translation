## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p133; it is the words *adiuge les biens dudit du Tilh, à la fille procreée de ſes œuures, & de ladite de Rols* from the *arrest* (texte-77), set over three printed lines with *fil-le* broken at the first line end, which the reflow keeps as three paragraphs; the English is set the same way, line by line, with the break placed at the corresponding word, and carries no marginal citations. Rendered as in texte-77: *adiuge les biens* → "adjudges the goods" (*biens* → goods per the glossary); *procreée de ſes œuures* → "procreated by his doings" per the glossary (*de ses œuvres*, Coras's periphrasis for fathering); *ladite de Rols* → "the said de Rols" per §3. The print's *a la fille* lacks the grave accent.
