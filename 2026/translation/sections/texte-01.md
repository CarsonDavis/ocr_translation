⟦p001⟧DECISION

OF THE PARLEMENT OF

Tholose, containing a memorable

and prodigious history, with

one hundred and eleven fine and learned anno-

tations, by Monsieur Master Jean

de Coras, reporting judge of the case.

Text of the Substance of the case,

and of the decision.

IN THE MONTH of January, one thousand five hundred and fifty-nine [= 1560 n.s.], Bertrande de Rols, of the place of Artigat, in the diocese of Rieux, presents herself as supplicant and plaintiff before the judge of Rieux, saying that twenty years, or thereabouts, may have passed since she, being a young girl of nine to ten years, was married to Martin Guerre, at that time also very young, and almost of the same age as the supplicant.
