⟦p000-title⟧MEMORABLE

DECISION

OF THE PARLEMENT

OF THOLOSE.

Containing

A prodigious History of a supposed

husband, which befell in our time: en-

riched with one hundred and eleven fine

and learned annota-

tions.

By Master Jean de Coras, Counsellor in the

Court, and reporting judge of the case.

Pronounced at the general sessions, the twelfth of

September. 1560.

AT PARIS,

For Galliot du Pré, at the first pillar

of the great hall of the Palace.

With the King's Privilege.

M. D. LXXII.
