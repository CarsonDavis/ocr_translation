⟦p031⟧And setting out the facts still more fully, he deduced that, having remained seven or eight years in the King's service in the wars, and some months in Spain to see the country, being desirous of seeing again his kinsfolk, his native land, Sanxi his child, and still more the said de Rols his wife, he had, three years ago and more, returned to the said place of Artigat.
