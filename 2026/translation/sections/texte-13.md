⟦p028⟧She had had a preliminary inquiry made into it by authority of the Judge of Rieux; and, claiming the whole to be verified, concluded against the said du Tilh for a double amend: the honourable (*amende honorable*, public penance), to ask pardon of God, of the King, and of the said de Rols, plaintiff, bareheaded and barefoot, in a shirt, holding a burning torch in his hands, saying that falsely, rashly and treacherously he has deceived, abused, betrayed and circumvented her, in taking the name and substituting the person of Martin Guerre her true husband, of which he repents, and asks her mercy for it; and for the profitable, in two thousand *livres*, and to costs, damages and interest.
