⟦p070⟧Which in all likelihood did not proceed from the said de Rols, for the reasons above; and considering likewise the good offices she had used towards the prisoner the night before. For immediately after he was retaken, she sent him his clothing, and money to live on.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p070, after Annotation XLVIII; the section carries no marginal citations. Reflow gap read through: *in continent* (*incontinent*). *Vray-ſemblablement* → "in all likelihood"; *ne procedoit de* → "did not proceed from"; *pour les raiſons que deſſus* → "for the reasons above"; *attendu meſmement* → "considering likewise"; *les offices, deſquelles elle auoit vſé enuers* → "the good offices she had used towards" (*office* feminine in the print; the kindnesses of the night related in texte-45); *reprins* → "retaken" (the second arrest, texte-46); *ſes accouſtremens* → "his clothing"; *de l'argent pour viure* → "money to live on". The case file §10 notes that on the same day as the complaint she sent the prisoner clothing and money — the fact Davis makes much of.
