⟦p037⟧And that his wife and sisters be confronted with him, assuring himself that they, who are all women of worth and honest, will recognise him; and that meanwhile the said de Rols, now being in the power of the said Pierre Guerre and dwelling in his house, be sequestered and placed in some house of good people, where she could not be seduced nor suborned. For the rest, concluding for his acquittal.
