⟦p073⟧Nor do the tokens which ⟦p074⟧the witnesses report count for anything: for by what is said above it is sufficiently answered that the witnesses have been validly objected to. Added to which, there are very few tokens given by them which are not found in the said accused, save for the height and the bulk; but as to that, the answer is easy: inasmuch as, as other witnesses not objected to depose, although the accused, when he departed, appeared taller, longer and more slender, nevertheless, since then, by the course of years, he would have filled out in body and grown stronger in the legs.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p073, after Annotation LIII; the section carries no marginal citations. The page break falls between *que* and *les teſmoins* and is kept at the corresponding words ("which ⟦p074⟧the witnesses"). *N'y ſont rien auſsi* → "Nor … count for anything" (as *n'y fait rien* at texte-50); *enſeignes* → "tokens" per the glossary (the particular marks the witnesses for du Tilh reported of him); *par ce deſſus* → "by what is said above"; *ſuffiſamment reſpondu* → "sufficiently answered"; *valablement reprochez* → "validly objected to" per §5; *Ioint, que* → "Joined, that" (as *Ioinct auſſi que* → "Joined also that" at annot-046); *ne ſe trouuent audit preuenu* → "are not found in the said accused"; *reſerué de la longueur, & groſſeur* → "save for the height and the bulk" (the body-shape evidence; cf. the case file §10 and texte-30, *trappe & fourni de corps*); *la reſponſe eſt aiſée* → "the answer is easy"; *teſmoins non reprochez* → "witnesses not objected to"; *quand partit* → "when he departed"; *reſſemblaſt plus haut, plus long, & plus greſle* → "appeared taller, longer and more slender" (*reſſembler* here in its old sense, "to seem"); *par le cours des ans* → "by the course of years"; *ſe ſeroit il rempli de corps, & renforcé de iambes* → "he would have filled out in body and grown stronger in the legs" (the reportive conditional of the record, kept as a conditional since the clause reports the witnesses' inference). Annotation LIV, which follows, supplies the classical examples of men grown fat with age.
