⟦p107⟧And do not excuse yourself by my sisters, nor by my Uncle: for there is no father, mother, Uncle, sisters nor brothers who ought better to know their son, nephew or brother than the wife ought to know the husband. And of the disaster which has befallen our house, no one is to blame but you. Whereupon the commissioners tried to excuse the said de Rols: but at this first meeting could never soften his heart, nor turn him from his austerity. Thus the imposture of the said du Tilh being entirely discovered, and the newcomer received by all as the only one, and recognised as Martin Guerre: and the trial by this means fully instructed ⟦p108⟧so as to be judged definitively, and the same having been reviewed, the Court, upon great and mature deliberation, pronounced the decision which follows.

DECISION.

SEEN the trial conducted by the Judge of Rieux against Arnauld du Tilh, called Pansette, calling himself Martin Guerre, prisoner in the Conciergerie, appellant from the said Judge, etc. It has been declared that the Court has set aside and sets aside the appeal of the said du Tilh, and the judgment appealed from, as null. And for the punishment and reparation of the imposture, forgery, substitution of name and person, adultery, rape, sacrilege, enslavement, theft and other offences committed by the said du Tilh, prisoner, resulting from the said trial, the Court has condemned and condemns him to make public penance (*amende honorable*) before the Church of the place of Artigat, and there, on his knees, in a shirt, bareheaded and barefoot, having the halter round his neck and holding in his hands a burning wax taper, to ask pardon of God, of the King, of Justice, and of the said Martin Guerre and de Rols, married persons: and this done, the said du Tilh shall be delivered into the hands of the executioner, who shall make him go the rounds through the accustomed streets and crossroads of the said place of Artigat: and, the halter round his neck, shall lead him before the house of the said Martin Guerre, ⟦p109⟧there to be hanged and strangled on a gallows which shall be erected there for that purpose, and afterwards his body burnt. And for certain causes and considerations moving the Court thereto, it has adjudged and adjudges the goods of the said du Tilh to the daughter procreated by his doings and of the said de Rols, under pretext of the marriage by him falsely pretended, substituting the name and person of the said Martin Guerre and by this means deceiving the said de Rols — the costs of Justice deducted. And moreover, it has dismissed and dismisses from the case and suit the said Martin Guerre and Bertrande de Rols, together with the said Pierre Guerre, uncle of the said Martin: and has remanded and remands the same du Tilh to the said Judge of Rieux, to have this present decision put into execution according to its form and tenor.

Pronounced judicially, the twelfth day of September, 1560.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p107 and carries no marginal citations. It continues Martin Guerre's speech to Bertrande begun in texte-76, closes the narrative, and gives the text of the *arrest* of 12 September 1560 under its own display head ARREST. (p108), followed by the display head of the last part of the book, EXPOSITION DES / Paroles de l'arreſt. (p109), translated line by line as the print sets it. The `⟨alt⟩` at *Oncle,⟨alt:Vncle,⟩* is taken as *Oncle*: the same word is printed *Oncle* two words earlier in the same sentence, and *Vncle* is not French. Reflow gaps read through: *com miſſaires*, *ſoy di ſant*, *ſup poſition*, *pro creée*, *pre texte*. Wrong sorts and slips translated by sense: *nouneau* for *nouueau*; *inſtruir* for *inſtruit*; *à adiugé* for *a adiugé*; *Prononce* for *Prononcé*; *deceuãt* with the tilde; the full stop after *Martin Guerre.* before *& par ce moyen* is run on. Renderings per the case file: *arreſt* → "decision" (never "judgment") and *ſentence* → "judgment", §5; *a mis, & met l'appellation … & ce dont a eſté appelé, au neant* → "has set aside and sets aside the appeal … and the judgment appealed from, as null", the fixed formula of §5, rendered so that texte-78 can quote its second half; *VEV le procés fait par le Iuge de Rieux à* → "SEEN the trial conducted by the Judge of Rieux against", the opening *Vu* of a French judgment; *Dit a eſté que* → "It has been declared that"; *impoſture, fauſſeté, ſuppoſition de nom, & perſonne, adultere, rapt, ſacrilege, plage, larrecin* → "imposture, forgery, substitution of name and person, adultery, rape, sacrilege, enslavement, theft" per §2 and the glossary (*plage* is the Roman *plagium*, not plagiarism; *rapt* covers abduction as well as rape, §9); *amende honnorable* → "public penance (*amende honorable*)" per §5; *teſte, & pieds nuds* → "bareheaded and barefoot"; *la hart au col* → "the halter round his neck"; *torche de cire ardente* → "a burning wax taper" as in §2; *executeur de la haute Iuſtice* → "the executioner" per §5; *faire les tours par les rues, & carrefours accouſtumez* → "go the rounds through the accustomed streets and crossroads" (the execution procession, §5 s.v. *criée*); *pendu, & eſtranglé* → "hanged and strangled", one act, not split; *procreée de ſes œuures* → "procreated by his doings" per the glossary; *fauſſement pretendu* → "falsely pretended" (*pretendu* in its older sense, claimed); *detraicts les frais de Iuſtice* → "the costs of Justice deducted"; *a mis, & met hors de procez, & inſtance* → "has dismissed and dismisses from the case and suit" per §5 *mis hors de cour / hors de procès*; *a renuoyé & renuoye* → "has remanded and remands"; *ſelon ſa forme & teneur* → "according to its form and tenor". In the narrative: *commiſſaires* → "commissioners" per annot-021; *amolir ſon cueur* → "soften his heart"; *le diuertir de ſon auſterité* → "turn him from his austerity"; *de tous vniquement receu* → "received by all as the only one" (*uniquement*, as the one and only Martin Guerre); *le procez … du tout inſtruit* → "the trial fully instructed"; *iceluy veu* → "the same having been reviewed", the *visite du procès* of §5; *à grande, & meure deliberation* → "upon great and mature deliberation".
