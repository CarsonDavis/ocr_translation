⟦p075⟧Still less can one allege the dissimilarity between the said accused and Sanxi Guerre, son of Martin: for besides that such judgments by resemblance (as has been often said above) are not very sure, there is a contrary summary inquiry upon the resemblance of the prisoner with the sisters of Martin Guerre, and one more probable, inasmuch as the likeness is with a greater number of persons, and such as are of like age, or very nearly, with him to whom the comparison is made.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p075, after Annotation LIIII; the section carries no marginal citations. It continues the record's answers to the points in the prisoner's favour. The `⟨alt⟩` at *diſsimili-⟨alt:diſſimili-⟩tude* is taken as either: both readings are *dissimilitude*, and the difference is only in the sort (*ſs* or *ſſ*). Reflow gap read through: *Guer re*. *Moins peut-on alleguer* → "Still less can one allege"; *diſſimilitude* → "dissimilarity"; *iugemens par ſemblance* → "judgments by resemblance"; *ne ſont pas fort aſſeurez* → "are not very sure"; *ſommaire apriſe* → "summary inquiry", the *sommaire aprise* glossed at texte-25, where the two inquiries into the resemblance of Sanxi and of the sisters were first recorded; *contraire* → "contrary", the inquiry that cuts the other way; *& plus probable* → "and one more probable", the contrary inquiry being the more probable of the two; *telles, qui ſont de pareil aage, ou peu ſ'en faut* → "such as are of like age, or very nearly"; *celuy à qui on fait la conference* → "him to whom the comparison is made" (*conferer* → to compare, per the glossary at annot-021).
