⟦p089⟧Seeing which, the commissioners think fit to ask, apart and in secret, of the ⟦p090⟧newcomer certain things among the most hidden, on which neither the one nor the other had yet been interrogated, nor on anything that came near them; which was done, and truly (as was since verified) answered by him. Afterwards, having had him withdrawn, they have the prisoner brought in, to whom they put the same interrogations, up to the number of ten or twelve, who answers in everything like the other. Which astonished the company, and made them fall into the opinion that the prisoner had something of magic — as indeed he was ill-famed for it in the said places of Artigat, Le Pin, Sagias and other neighbouring places.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p089, after Annotation LXIX (the print's LXX); the section carries no marginal citations. The page break falls between *au* and *nouueau venu* and is kept at the corresponding word ("the ⟦p090⟧newcomer"). Wrong sorts and slips translated by sense: *quelqes* for *quelques*; *esbahir* with a round *s*; a lower-case *ce* opening the sentence after the full stop. Reflow is clean. *Quoy voyant* → "Seeing which"; *les commiſſaires* → "the commissioners" (the judges delegated to examine, per the glossary at annot-021); *ſ'aduiſent de* → "think fit to"; *à part & en ſecret* → "apart and in secret" (the separate and secret examination required by Villers-Cotterêts art. 162, case file §5 — and the convergence of the two men's answers under separate examination, which the case file §10 marks as a loaded fact); *des plus cachees* → "among the most hidden"; *ni de choſe qui en approchaſt* → "nor on anything that came near them"; *veritablement (comme depuis fut verifié) reſpondu* → "truly (as was since verified) answered"; *l'ayant fait tirer* → "having had him withdrawn"; *font venir le priſonnier* → "they have the prisoner brought in"; *iuſqu'au nombre de dix ou douze interrogatoires* → "up to the number of ten or twelve interrogations" (*interrogatoire* here of the individual questions put, §5); *reſpond en tout comme l'autre* → "answers in everything like the other"; *fit esbahir la compagnie* → "astonished the company" (*esbahir* → to astonish per the glossary; *la compagnie*, the assembled judges); *tomber en opinion* → "fall into the opinion"; *euſt quelque choſe de la magie* → "had something of magic" (*magie* → magic per the glossary; the suspicion Annotation LXX treats at length); *il en eſtoit diffamé* → "he was ill-famed for it" (*diffamé*, in the older sense, of one who has a bad name for something — not "defamed" in the modern sense of slandered); *eſdits lieux d'Artigat, du Pin, de Sagias* → "in the said places of Artigat, Le Pin, Sagias" (the three places where the *monitoire* was published, texte-24; *du Pin* → Le Pin and *Sagias* kept, per §4); *circonuoiſins* → "neighbouring". The present tenses of the record (*ſ'aduiſent*, *font venir*, *reſpond*) are kept.
