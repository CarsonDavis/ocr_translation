⟦p021⟧And to whom the said du Tilh had given several private and particular tokens, even of the acts and words which pass most secretly between married people, and which others cannot well know or understand — so far as to tell her the places, times and hours of the secret acts of marriage (much easier to understand than honest to recite or write), and the words which, before, after and during the act, they had exchanged — had likewise persuaded herself, with the others, that the said du Tilh was certainly Martin Guerre her husband.
