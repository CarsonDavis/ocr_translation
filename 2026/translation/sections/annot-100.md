⟦p142⟧It was fitting to remand the execution of the decision to the Judge of Rieux, who had not spared himself in searching out, by all honest means of Justice, the truth of this deed. Joined that it greatly befits the dignity of the sovereign courts to maintain and preserve the authority of the inferior ones {a}, and so to act that, by their example and for the public good, all Judges (each in his degree) be by all and everywhere revered, as our Laws also often command {b}.

TEXT OF THE RECORD

of the execution.

Afterwards, to execute the said decision, the same du Tilh was brought back from the Conciergerie to the place of Artigat, where the execution was to ⟦p143⟧be done, and there was heard by the said Judge of Rieux.

## Notes
The print heads this annotation ANNOTAT. C. on p142; the transcription numbers it 99. It expounds the words of remand (texte-98), and then — within the same section as the transcription divides it — the print opens the second part of the book with the display head *TEXTE DV PROCES de l'execution*, the record of the execution of 16 September 1560 that the 1565 edition added with the last eleven annotations (case file §1). The head is translated line by line as the print sets it: "TEXT OF THE RECORD / of the execution" (*procès* → "the record" per the glossary). The paragraph that follows it is the first *Texte* of the execution record, kept in this file because the transcription keeps it here. Markers a–b on p142, both served. One page break, inside *de|uoit faire* (p142/p143), kept at "was to ⟦p143⟧be done". Three `⟨alt⟩` markers, listed in the report; none changes the English. *Renuoyer* → "remand" (texte-77, texte-98); *honneſtes moyens de Iuſtice* → "honest means of Justice"; *cours ſouueraines* → "sovereign courts" (annot-073); *les inferieurs* → "the inferior ones" (the lower courts); *chacun en ſon degré* → "each in his degree"; *reuerez* → "revered"; *conciergerie* → "Conciergerie" per the glossary; *fut ouy* → "was heard" (*ouïr* → to hear, case file §5).

- {a} (p142): **Code 7.64 (*Quando provocare non est necesse*), l. *Omnem* (fragment not identified); Decretals, X 2.28 (*De appellationibus*), c. *Ut debitus* (Lateran IV, c. 35; cf. X 2.28.59, chapter number not verified)** — l. omnẽ. C. quand. prouo non eſt nece. c. vt debitus. de appel. [*Ut debitus honor iudicibus et sedibus servetur* — that due honour be kept to judges and their seats: the constitution restraining frivolous appeals from lower judges. The transcribers' *e.* for the siglum is read *c.*, a chapter of the Decretals.]
- {b} (p142): **Digest 16.3 (*Depositi vel contra*), l. 1, § *Casum* (paragraph not verified); Digest 1.18 (*De officio praesidis*), l. *Observandum* (D. 1.18.19, Callistratus)** — l. i. P. caſum D. depoſiti l. obſeruandum D. de officio præſ. [Callistratus: the magistrate dispensing justice must make himself easy of access but not suffer himself to be despised — *contemni non patiatur*. The transcribers' *depoſt.*/*depoſit.* is the title *Depositi* abbreviated.]
