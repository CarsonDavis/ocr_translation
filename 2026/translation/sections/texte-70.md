⟦p088⟧Nevertheless, that he shall be confronted with the said du Tilh, prisoner, with Pierre Guerre, Bertrande de Rols and the sisters of the said Martin, together with certain other witnesses who were the principal of those who had so pertinaciously assured that the prisoner was truly Martin Guerre. He is heard; he sets down and gives tokens upon the same interrogations ⟦p089⟧that had been put to the said prisoner — not, however, so certain, so proper, in so great a number, nor of such multitude as the said accused had given. Afterwards he is confronted with the said du Tilh, prisoner, who shows himself more obstinate than ever, calling the said Martin, the newcomer, a cheat, a wicked man, a beggarly knave; submitting himself moreover, under penalty of being hanged, to make good that this newcomer had been bought for ready money and instructed by Pierre Guerre — not, however, so well that he would not confound him and clearly demonstrate the substitution. And upon this he begins to discourse, and to interrogate him about several things that had passed in the house of the said Martin Guerre; whereupon, in truth, the newcomer did not satisfy so well as the prisoner had done, and was still doing.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p088, after Annotation LXVIII (the print's LXIX); the section carries no marginal citations. It continues the Court's order of texte-66 (*La cour ordonne … Neantmoins qu'il ſera confronté*), and the opening clause is left dependent, as in the French. The page break falls between *interrogatoires* and *qu'on* and is kept at the corresponding words ("interrogations ⟦p089⟧that"). Wrong sorts and slips translated by sense: *deſtre* for *d'eſtre*; *Martin guerre* with a lower-case *g*. Reflow is clean. *Aud. du Tilh priſonnier* → "the said du Tilh, prisoner"; *ſeurs dudit Martin* → "the sisters of the said Martin"; *les principaux de ceux* → "the principal of those"; *ſi pertinacement aſſeuré* → "so pertinaciously assured" (*pertinacité* → pertinacity in the glossary); *Il eſt ouy* → "He is heard" (*estre ouy*, §5); *conſigne, & baille enſeignes* → "he sets down and gives tokens" (*consigner* → to set down, *enseignes* → tokens, per the glossary); *ſur les meſmes interrogatoires* → "upon the same interrogations" (*interrogatoire* → interrogation, §5); *ſi certaines, ſi propres* → "so certain, so proper" (so exactly fitting); *de telle numeroſité* → "of such multitude" (*numerosité* → multitude at annot-021); *ledit preuenu* → "the said accused" (*prevenu* → the accused, §5; Coras uses *prevenu* and *prisonnier* interchangeably of du Tilh); *plus obſtiné que iamais* → "more obstinate than ever"; *affronteur, meſchant, beliſtre* → "a cheat, a wicked man, a beggarly knave" (*affronteur* → cheat, *meschant* → wicked per the glossary; *bélître* is a beggar and, by extension, a worthless rogue); *ſe ſubmettant en outre à peine deſtre pendu, qu'il iuſtifieroit* → "submitting himself moreover, under penalty of being hanged, to make good that" (*justifier* in the legal sense of proving one's allegation; he offers to stake his neck on it); *acheté à deniers contans* → "bought for ready money"; *inſtruit* → "instructed" (coached; cf. *tomber en instruction* at texte-41); *qu'il ne le confondiſt & demonſtraſt clairement la ſuppoſition* → "that he would not confound him and clearly demonstrate the substitution" (*il* is the prisoner, who claims he will expose the newcomer; *supposition* → substitution per the glossary); *commence diſcourir* → "begins to discourse"; *l'interroguer* → "to interrogate him"; *ne ſatisfaiſoit pas ſi bien* → "did not satisfy so well"; *& faiſoit encores* → "and was still doing". The newcomer's weaker showing is the fact Annotation LXIX marvels at.
