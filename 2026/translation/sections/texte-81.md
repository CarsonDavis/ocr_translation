⟦p109⟧And the judgment appealed from, as null.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p109, the first of the fragments of the *arrest* that Coras expounds word by word in the last part of the book; it carries no marginal citations. The words are the second half of the formula *a mis, & met l'appellation dudit du Tilh, & ce dont a eſté appelé, au neant* in texte-77, and the English repeats the rendering given there ("the judgment appealed from, as null"), so that the exposition quotes the decision in the same words as the decision itself. *Ce dont a eſté appelé* is literally "that from which appeal was taken", which the next annotation identifies as the judgment (*ſentence*) of the judge of Rieux.
