⟦p150⟧He confessed also that he was debtor to several persons, whom he names in his hearing, in divers sums of money, quantities of wheat, wine and millet, and moreover in certain quintals of wool, more fully specified therein; re⟦p151⟧questing that the said creditors be satisfied out of the property that he still has at the place of Le Pin, as much from his late father Arnauld Guilhem du Tilh as from others — now possessed and occupied by Carbon Barrau his maternal uncle, whom, by reason of this, he has already sued.

## Notes
The print heads this section TEXTE. on p150. The body carries no marker and the margin no note. The page break falls inside *re|querant*; kept at "re⟦p151⟧questing". Reflow gap read through: *quin taux*. *Eſtre debiteur à pluſieurs* → "that he was debtor to several persons"; *en ſon audition* → "in his hearing" (*audition* → hearing, the accused's examination, texte-21); *en diuerſes ſommes d'argent* → "in divers sums of money"; *bled* → "wheat"; *millet* → "millet"; *& neãtmoins, en certains quintaux de laine* → "and moreover in certain quintals of wool" — *neantmoins* here continues the list rather than opposing it, so the concessive "nevertheless" is not used; *plus au long y ſpecifiez* → "more fully specified therein", i.e. in the record of the hearing; *requerant leſdits creanciers eſtre ſatisfaits* → "requesting that the said creditors be satisfied"; *du bien qu'il a encore au lieu du Pin* → "out of the property that he still has at the place of Le Pin" (*biens* → property; Le Pin per the case file §4 — in du Tilh country near Sajas, not near Artigat); *ſon feu pere Arnauld Guilhem du Tilh* → "his late father Arnauld Guilhem du Tilh" (§3, §8); *Carbon Barrau ſon oncle maternel* → "Carbon Barrau his maternal uncle" (§3); *mis en inſtance* → "sued" per the glossary (texte-14). The suit against Barrau is the *procez* annot-107 discusses, and the ground of the objection du Tilh raised against his uncle at the confrontations.
