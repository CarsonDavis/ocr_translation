⟦p045⟧The confrontations completed, and objections [*reproches*] delivered by the said du Tilh, and a petition that he be permitted to publish a monitory letter [*monitoire*: letters read from the pulpit, calling on all who know anything to reveal it on pain of excommunication] upon the articles thereto attached concerning the pretended subornation of the said de Rols, by order he is admitted to verify the objections by him deduced; and nevertheless, considering the matter in question, to have the said monitory letter published, the better to know the truth; and it is ordered that inquiry shall be made on the court's own motion, as well in the places of Le Pin, Sagias and Artigat as in others neighbouring and necessary, as much upon the ⟦p046⟧verification and recognition of the said prisoner, calling himself Martin Guerre, as upon the life and repute of the witnesses confronted. The monitory letter published, the revelations re-taken [*resumees*: the depositions made under the *monitoire*, repeated before the judge], the inquiries on the court's own motion made, there results, among other things, that the said de Rols has all the time of her life, and even during the absence of the said Martin her husband, lived virtuously and honourably.
