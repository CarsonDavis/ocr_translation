## Glossary decisions

1. **bruit & fame → "rumour and common repute"; bruit & renommée → "rumour and renown".** The §9 seed row, annot-041, annot-053, annot-062 and texte-62 already read so. annot-002 had the two swapped (*bruit & renommée* → "rumour and common repute", *bruit & fame ſuffiroit* → "rumour and repute") and annot-060 dropped "common". Sections touched: annot-002 (two places), annot-060. The annot-002 row in §9 is corrected.
2. **Et de dire que … → "And as to saying that …"** — the record's formula for taking up the appellant's point. texte-56 and texte-58 (*car de dire premierement*) have the literal form; texte-62's "And as to the argument that" is brought in line and its §9 row amended.
3. **ſainctement → "holily"** (annot-062, annot-083). The annot-083 row had settled it; the annot-062 row hedged ("reviewer may prefer holily"). Coras's word is religious and "wisely" lost the colour. Section touched: annot-062.
4. **Ioinct que / ioint que → "Added to which"** (texte-58, texte-66). texte-58 read "Joined that". Section touched: texte-58.

## The ten most consequential fixes

1. **texte-80** (p107) — *& le nouneau venu de tous vniquement receu, & recognu pour Martin Guerre*
   Was: "and the newcomer received by all as the only one, and recognised as Martin Guerre"
   Now: "and the newcomer received and recognised by all as the sole Martin Guerre"
   *vniquement* qualifies both verbs; the old split left "the only one" dangling.

2. **texte-63** (p083) — *Auſsi ne ſe pouuoit on perſonnément fonder a la cognoiſſance que le priſonnier auoit*
   Was: "Nor could one firmly [unclear: *perſonnément*, as printed — not a known word; perhaps *pertinemment*, "pertinently", or *fermement*] found oneself upon"
   Now: "Nor could one personally [unclear: *perſonnément*, as printed — Middle French *personnement*, "in person, personally", which Ringold–Lewis also read; or a misprint for *pertinemment*] found oneself upon"
   *personnement* is an attested Middle French adverb; the flag's "not a known word" was wrong and "firmly" rested on a guess. The flag stays because a misprint remains possible.

3. **texte-72** (p097) — *pour leur eſtre reſpectiuement & enſemblément exhibez*
   Was: "so that the two men might be exhibited to them severally and together"
   Now: "so that [the two men] might be exhibited to them severally and together"
   The French has no subject; the supplied one must be bracketed.

4. **annot-082** (p113) — *il fut par le commandement des Senateurs fait priſonnier, & dans la maiſon executé à mort*
   Was: "and in the prison-house executed to death"
   Now: "and in the house [the prison, in Valerius Maximus] executed to death"
   "prison" comes from Val. Max. 9.15.1 (*in carcere*), not from the print.

5. **annot-002** (outside the quarter) — *preuue par bruit & renommée* / *bruit & fame ſuffiroit*
   Was: "proof by rumour and common repute" / "by rumour and repute, would suffice"
   Now: "proof by rumour and renown" / "by rumour and common repute, would suffice"
   The two pairs were swapped against the house rule.

6. **annot-060** (p080) — *comme eſt vne iuriſdiction, election de ſepulture, bruit & fame*
   Was: "such as a jurisdiction, a choice of burial, rumour and repute"
   Now: "such as a jurisdiction, a choice of burial, rumour and common repute"

7. **texte-62** (p082) — *Et de dire, que par les enqueſtes eſt rapporté le bruit*
   Was: "And as to the argument that by the inquiries it is reported"
   Now: "And as to saying that by the inquiries it is reported"

8. **texte-58** (p077) — *ioint que la negation qu'ils font*
   Was: "Joined that the negation they make"
   Now: "Added to which, the negation they make"

9. **annot-062** (p083) — *Dont nos loix ont ſainctement ordonné*
   Was: "Wherefore our laws have wisely ordained"
   Now: "Wherefore our laws have holily ordained"

10. **texte-78** (p105) — *elle preſenta requeſte à ladite cour*
    Was: "she presented a petition to the said court"
    Now: "she presented a petition to the said Court"
    The only lower-case "court" for the Parlement left in the book's English.

Also: annot-082 "There is also talk of" → "Mention is also made of" (register); annot-082 "the said St John's" → "the said Saint John's" (the print spells out *Sainct Ieã*; pass-0 rule).

## Ringold–Lewis

Real divergences, all decided from the French and all in our favour: texte-58 ("in addition to denying" for *ioint que la negation … vient en preuue*); texte-60 ("far from being a thousand" for *encor qu'ils fuſſent mille*); texte-61 ("broken teeth" for *ſoubre-dents*); texte-70 ("being sworn" for *conſigne*); texte-71 ("the justices" for *commiſſaires*); texte-75 ("augmented by" for *ſurmontee des*); texte-80 ("granted, performed and nullified" for *mis au neant*, "the Court" for *à Iuſtice*, "executioner of the High Court" for *haute Iuſtice*). At texte-63 R&L's "personally" turns out to be right and we now agree. At texte-67 both translations read *en preuention de meſme faict* literally although texte-47 says the Seneschal's charge was different; Coras's inconsistency, for the apparatus.
