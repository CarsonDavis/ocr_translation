- **annot-084** Coras's "returned from exile" for Cicero's divorce is kept with a remark; Plutarch's chronology differs.
- **annot-086** *diæteres* / *directaire*: Latin kept in italics in the body with one bracketed gloss. If the house prefers an English word throughout, "chamber-thief / house-breaker" would serve, at the cost of Coras's point about the variant reading.
