## `[unclear]` passages

One.

- **texte-60** (p083): `Auſsi ne ſe pouuoit on perſonnément fonder a la cognoiſſance que le priſornier auoit…` — *perſonnément* is not a French word. Translated "Nor could one firmly [unclear: …] found oneself upon the knowledge…", with the flag in the text. The context wants an adverb of degree or warrant; *pertinemment* ("pertinently, on good ground") or *fermement* ("firmly") would both fit, and a misread or wrong-sort *pertinemment* seems the likelier. A transcriber's look at the image would settle it: the word is on the first lines of the p083 TEXTE block.
