## Glossary decisions (in full)

1. **Ioinct que / Ioint auſſi que → "Added to which" / "Added to which also"** everywhere.
   The pass-3 ruling named texte-58 and texte-66 only; five places still read "Joined
   (also) that". Touched: texte-54, annot-046, annot-098 (×2), annot-100.
2. **Et de dire que → "And as to saying that"** in the annotations as in the record.
   Touched: annot-095 ("as to the argument that"), annot-097 ("as for saying that"),
   annot-109 ("to say that").
3. **Mais quoy? → "But what!"** everywhere (the §9 row's form, already at texte-26,
   annot-082, annot-083). Touched: annot-001, annot-110 ("But what of it?").
4. **cas (as a word of the decision) → "offences"**, texte-80's rendering. Touched:
   texte-89 ("And other cases,"), annot-088.
5. **nul ne ſçait → "no one is ignorant of"** at annot-104, the pass-1 ruling applied
   to the occurrence it missed.
6. **n'y fait rien (de) dire que → "it is nothing to say that" / "nor is it anything to
   say that"** (annot-086's form). Touched: annot-108, annot-109.
7. **affrontemens (countable) → "acts of cheating"** (the §9 row). Touched: texte-107.
8. **M. Cicerõ → "Marcus Cicero"** (pass-0 praenomen rule). Touched: annot-109.
9. **la ſentence du iuge (generic) → "the judgment of the judge"** (§5). Touched: annot-111.
10. **le xvj. Septembre → "the sixteenth of September"**, spelled out as the record's
    other dates are. Touched: texte-102.

## The ten most consequential fixes

1. **annot-083** (p119). Fr: *Il ſemble à pluſieurs qu'ils n'ayent point fait la peine de
   ce crime capitale: voire que ne l'ayent voulu punir d'vn ſimple banniſſement: ſi ce
   n'eſt quand l'adultere eſtoit conioint auec inceſte.*
   Was: "nay, that they did not wish to punish it with a simple banishment, unless when
   the adultery was joined with incest." Now: "nay, that they wished to punish it only
   with a simple banishment, unless when the adultery was joined with incest." The old
   English made incest earn mere banishment; the Roman rule (and note d) is the reverse.
2. **annot-104** (p146). Fr: *Surquoy nul ne ſçait la differẽce entre tragedie, &
   comedie.* Was: "Whereupon: no one knows the difference between tragedy and comedy."
   Now: "Whereupon, no one is ignorant of the difference …" — Coras's idiom (pass-1),
   and he goes on to expound the difference he says everyone knows.
3. **texte-89 / annot-088** (p127). Fr: *Et autre cas* / *Les autres cas, ſont pluſieurs
   autres affrontemens.* Was: "And other cases," / "The other cases are …". Now: "And
   other offences," / "The other offences are …", matching the decision at texte-80.
4. **annot-095** (p133). Fr: *tels enfans ne ſuccedent à pere, meres, & autres leurs
   parens.* Was: "succeed to father, mothers and their other kinsfolk". Now: "succeed to
   father and mother, and to their other kinsfolk".
5. **annot-111** (p158). Fr: *ceux, qui meinent à fin, & effectuent la ſentence du
   iuge.* Was: "effect the sentence of the judge". Now: "effect the judgment of the
   judge" (§5: *sentence* is never "sentence").
6. **annot-109** (p155). Fr: *Et de dire que la confeſſion d'vne debte … fait preuue
   ſuffiſante. Cela ſ'entẽd, dol & fraude ceſſans.* Was: "And to say that … — that is
   understood where …". Now: "And as to saying that … : that is understood where …".
7. **annot-098** (p138). Fr: *Ioint auſſi (qui ſera pour la ſeconde raiſon) que durant
   ces trois ans …* Was: "Joined also (…) that during these three years". Now: "Added
   to which also (…), during these three years". Likewise *Ioint que comme dit S.
   Ambroiſe* → "Added to which, as St Ambrose says".
8. **annot-110** (p158). Fr: *Mais quoy? en Frãce nous ſommes hors de ceſte diſpute.*
   Was: "But what of it? In France …". Now: "But what! In France …" (with annot-001).
9. **texte-107** (p150). Fr: *pluſieurs larrecins, & affrontemens.* Was: "several
   thefts and cheats". Now: "several thefts and acts of cheating". (Ringold–Lewis:
   "thefts and outrages", wrong.)
10. **texte-102** (p144). Fr: *le xvj. Septembre audit an 1560.* Was: "on the 16th of
    September". Now: "on the sixteenth of September".

## Ringold–Lewis (TEXTE sections of the execution record and the words of the decision)

Divergences, all recorded as findings and none moving our English:

- texte-103: R–L "Pierre de Guilhet, inhabitant of Mane" — *hoſte* is innkeeper (cf.
  texte-32), and our print reads *Guilher*; they also drop *maiſtre*. Ours right.
- texte-105: both supply the missing verb *[ſouloit]*; R–L "as cleverly" for *cautement*,
  ours "as warily" — interpretive.
- texte-107: R–L "outrages" for *affrontemens*. Ours right.
- texte-108: R–L "whom in this way he had already placed in authority" for *mis en
  inſtance* (= sued). Ours right.
- texte-88, 89, 98, 101, 111: "robbery", "other such offenses", "excluded and excludes",
  "dismissed and sends", "will" — house-rule differences (§5, §9); 89 supports our
  decision cas → offences.
