## `[unclear]` passages

None. Two readings that stop short of a flag but that the reviewer should know about:

- **annot-093** (p130–p131): `Les meſchans auſſi ne euident pas moins, que ſous ce manteau…` — *euident* is no French verb; read as *cuident* (*cuider*, "to believe", glossary annot-010) by the wrong sort *e* for *c*, and translated "the wicked also believe no less that…". The sense fits the parallel with *les imperites … penſent couurir*.
- **annot-092** (p130): Ovid's line is printed `aurifera, reſte pependit aqua`; the manuscripts and editions read *teste* ("with the gold-bearing water as witness"). Translated as printed ("hung by a rope over the gold-bearing water"), since *reste* (ablative of *restis*) construes, and the variant is noted in the section file. A reviewer may prefer the editions' reading in the English.
