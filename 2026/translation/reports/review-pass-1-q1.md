## Glossary decisions

1. **nul ne ſçait** (annot-005, annot-016) → "no one is ignorant of" / "there is no one who does not know". Coras's idiom carries the force of *nul n'ignore*: annot-016 has the full form *nul ne ſçait … qu'elle ne ſoit extremement grande*, which the translator already rendered "there is no one who does not know … that it is exceedingly great"; annot-005's elliptical *nul ne ſçait (comme ie croy) l'hiſtoire de Iacob* is the same idiom, and its `[unclear]` flag is resolved on it. New §9 row. (annot-104, outside the quarter, has a third instance that is genuinely ambiguous; recorded as a finding for Q4.)
2. **femmes de bien & honneſtes** → "women of worth and honest" wherever the phrase recurs. The §9 row settled texte-39; texte-20 and annot-020 still read "good and honest". Row extended, two sections brought into line.
3. **enſorcellement & malefice** → "bewitchment and sorcery". The §9 row settled the pair at annot-022; texte-24 read "bewitchment and spell". Row extended, one section brought into line.

## The fixes, with French and both Englishes

1. **annot-005** (p019), Horace, *Carm.* 4.5. French print: *Nullus polluitur caſta domus ſtupris.* Was: "No house is polluted by unchaste defilements". Now: "The chaste house is polluted by no defilements". *Casta* qualifies *domus*; the print's *Nullus* is for *nullis*.
2. **annot-016** (p032), Propertius. French print: *Omnis amor magnꝰ, ſed apertè in cõiuge maior.* Was: "All love is great, but greater in an avowed spouse." Now: "All love is great, but plainly greater in a spouse." *Aperte* is the adverb.
3. **annot-005** (p017). *Sur quoy nul ne ſçait (comme ie croy) l'hiſtoire de Iacob.* Was: "On which no one (as I believe) knows not [unclear: …]". Now: "On which no one (as I believe) is ignorant of the history of Jacob [*nul ne ſçait*, Coras's idiom for *nul n'ignore*, as at annotation XVI]". Flag resolved (decision 1).
4. **annot-002** (p007). *Vray eſt, que les meſmes autheurs entendent bien, que les nouuelles ſeront ttouuées aſſez certaines.* Was: "the same authors well intend that the news shall be found certain enough". Now: "well understand that". *Entendre que* is "understand/mean", not "intend".
5. **texte-23** (p044). *& nõme les preſtres, & que l'vn d'eux (qu'elle con ſignoit) luy fit manger…* Was: "and she names the priests, and says that one of them". Now: "and [says] that one of them". The French has no second verb; the supplied word is now bracketed.
6. **texte-20** (p037). *qu'elles, qui ſont tou tes femmes de bien, & honeſtes, le recog noiſtront.* Was: "who are all good and honest women". Now: "who are all women of worth and honest" (decision 2). R&L: "who were all good and honorable women".
7. **annot-020** (p038). *elle doit eſtre commiſe à quelque femme de bien, & honneſte.* Was: "to some good and honest woman". Now: "to some woman of worth and honest" (decision 2).
8. **texte-24** (p045). *Ledit du Tilh ouy ſur cet enſorcellement & malefice.* Was: "heard upon this bewitchment and spell". Now: "heard upon this bewitchment and sorcery" (decision 3). R&L: "questioned on this sorcery and evil spell".

## Ringold–Lewis, in brief

All ten divergences are R&L errors or losses, not ours: *rendu compte & preſtation de reliqua* read as accomplished (texte-14); *ſa patrie* dropped (texte-16); *coup de barre* flattened to "hard blow" (texte-18); the talion argument garbled with "he (Pierre)" (texte-19); *accarer* given as "brought to him" / "held" (texte-20, texte-22); *de la partie des Baſcouz* made the agent of verification (texte-21); *il eſt receu à verifier* as "by law he is required" (texte-25); *amplié l'arreſt* as "announced" (texte-26); *dommages* dropped (texte-13). Everywhere else the two versions agree in meaning, including every witness count.
