# Citation check, batch q1

The Notes entries in batch q1 whose identification was marked "unverified", "??", "?" or "unidentified". Each one was checked against the source text. Only the bold identification was rewritten, and only when the passage was found. The sigla after the em-dash were never touched.

Sources used:
- Roman law: the Krüger and Mommsen text at droitromain.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr. For the Digest, the vulgate numbering is given in parentheses where Mommsen records it.
- Canon law: the OCR of Friedberg's *Corpus iuris canonici* (Graz reprint, 1955) on archive.org: BD1141951 (Decretum) and BD1141952 (Decretals).
- Classical texts: the Latin Library, for Valerius Maximus and Solinus.
- Fregoso: the 1518 OCR on archive.org.

Where the source is an OCR, the matched words are quoted in the OCR's own spelling, normalised only lightly.

Two patterns are worth knowing for later batches:
- **Decretum C. 33 q. 1 is often misnumbered in our notes.** The order is c. 1 *Quod autem interrogasti*, c. 2 *De his requisisti*, c. 3 *Si quis accepit*, c. 4 *Si per sortiarias*. Earlier notes had *Requisisti* as c. 1 and *Si per sortiarias* as c. 2. Other sections citing these canons should be checked.
- **"c. final. de frigid." is X 4.15.7 *Litterae*, not *Laudabilem*.** *Laudabilem* is c. 5 of X 4.15, which has 7 chapters.
