## 6. Notes for the editor

- annot-083 {q}: the incipit fix (*Dilecto*, VI 5.11.6) is secure palaeographically and the title has no other candidate, but the chapter (a dean of Orléans defending himself by censure against a bailiff) is an odd authority for "adultery punished by excommunication only"; Coras may be citing it for the general point that the Church's sword is censure.
- annot-039 {b}: *Etiam matris* exists nowhere in the Code; the likeliest explanations are a wrong sort for a law of C. 4.19 beginning otherwise, or a gloss-word taken for an incipit. Left open.
- annot-022 {p}: Coras's *§ Si vero* for the impotence rule has no match in Nov. 22 as the Authenticum gives it; the matter is c. 6 *Per occasionem*. Possibly he cites from a gloss or from Nov. 117 c. 12, which repeats the three-year rule — not checked beyond Nov. 117's incipits (none *Si vero* on this).
- Crinito: the 1543 edition is again one chapter off Coras's number (XXI.9 vs printed XXI.10), as q3 noted for VIII and XVII.
